Several airlines are still waiving change fees on many tickets to, from, or through Northeast airports this weekend as a coastal storm keeps wind and flood warnings in effect through Sunday. Saturday morning FAA boards at Newark (EWR), LaGuardia (LGA), Boston (BOS), and Philadelphia (PHL) showed only light delays. That is general airport status, so check your flight.

This is the same check-your-booking habit as [Monday's FAA equipment outage](/news/newark-jfk-laguardia-flights-resume-faa-outage/) and [August's Chicago storm waivers](/news/chicago-ohare-storm-ground-stops-waivers/). This weekend's windows cover storm travel on many of the same airports.

## What happened

The [National Weather Service in New York](https://www.weather.gov/okx/) still had a coastal flood warning through 6 p.m. Eastern on Sunday, September 27th. For Staten Island, that warning included two to three feet of inundation in vulnerable waterfront areas. A wind advisory for New York City, Long Island, and coastal Connecticut remains in effect until 6 a.m. Sunday, with north winds of 25 to 35 mph and gusts up to 55 mph (about 40 to 56 km/h, gusts up to 89 km/h).

[Air Canada's September 24th notice](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/northeast-us-weather-event.html) described a significant storm with strong winds, heavy rain, and coastal flooding across the mid-Atlantic and northeastern U.S. this weekend.

As of about 9:31 a.m. Eastern on Saturday, September 26th, FAA airport pages at [Newark](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=EWR&p=1), [LaGuardia](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=LGA&p=1), [Boston](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=BOS&p=1), and [Philadelphia](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=PHL&p=1) showed no destination delays and general delays of 15 minutes or less. [JFK](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=JFK&p=1) had departure delays of 16 to 30 minutes from a traffic-volume restriction. Those pages are not flight-specific.

## What this means for travelers

If you are booked to, from, or through a listed Northeast airport on Saturday or Sunday, check **your flight** before you leave for the airport. The storm is still producing coastal flooding and gusty winds through Sunday. Aircraft, crews, and connecting passengers can still end up in the wrong cities even when a hub board looks quiet.

Airport lists differ by airline. Delta's posted cities do not include Boston. Southwest posted LaGuardia, Boston, Long Island/Islip, and smaller New England airports. Open manage booking for the version that applies to your ticket.

If you are still booking a trip after this weekend, nothing material should change.

Canadian itineraries that connect at Newark, JFK, LaGuardia, Boston, or Philadelphia take the same hit as a US domestic ticket.

## Airline waivers still in play

Rules differ by ticket purchase date, original travel date, allowed new dates, cabin, and city pair. Open manage booking before you pay a change fee.

| American | September 25th-27th | September 27th |
| Delta | September 26th-27th | September 30th |
| JetBlue | September 25th-27th | September 30th |
| Southwest | September 25th-27th | within 14 days of original date |

[American's severe weather alert](https://www.aa.com/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) (current as of September 25th) covers American-operated flights in any fare class, including Basic Economy, to, from, or through 16 airports: Baltimore (BWI), Bangor (BGR), Boston, Hartford (BDL), Manchester (MHT), JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Norfolk (ORF), Philadelphia, Portland, Maine (PWM), Providence (PVD), Richmond (RIC), Washington Dulles (IAD), Washington Reagan (DCA), and White Plains (HPN). Tickets must have been bought by September 23rd. New travel must be September 24th through 29th between the same cities, in the same cabin or with a fare difference. Changes must be booked by September 27th.

[Delta's Northeast weather flexibility page](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/weather-alerts/northeast-weather-flexibility) covers travel to, from, or through White Plains, Providence, Portland, Maine, Philadelphia, Newark, LaGuardia, and JFK on September 26th and 27th. Tickets must have been issued on or before September 25th. You can change the flight on or before September 30th, and the new departure must also be on or before September 30th, or cancel for an eCredit. Boston, Baltimore, and the Washington airports are not on that Delta city list.

[JetBlue's travel alerts](https://www.jetblue.com/travelalerts) waive change and cancel fees and fare differences for JetBlue-operated flights on September 25th through 27th to, from, or through Boston, Hyannis (HYA), Long Island MacArthur (ISP), Martha's Vineyard (MVY), Nantucket (ACK), JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Providence, White Plains, and Worcester (ORH). Original travel must have been booked before September 24th. You may rebook through September 30th in manage trips before the original departure. Group bookings of 10 or more need to call.

[Southwest's Northeast advisory](https://www.southwest.com/travel-advisory/) covers Boston, Hartford, Long Island MacArthur, Manchester, LaGuardia, Portland, Maine, and Providence on September 25th through 27th. Eligible customers may rebook or travel standby within 14 days of the original travel date between the original city pairs without paying a change in airfare, or keep the existing reservation. If Southwest cancels or significantly delays the flight and you choose not to travel, refund rules on that page still apply.

[WestJet's strong-winds advisory](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/get-travel-ready/advisories), posted September 24th, covers travel to or from LaGuardia, JFK, and Boston on September 25th through 27th. For flight-only bookings made directly with WestJet, you can change or cancel in Manage Trips. If you booked through an agent or another website, contact that source.

Air Canada said it implemented a free-change policy for travel to, from, or via airports in the storm area. Confirm the cities and dates that apply to your ticket in manage booking or the [Daily Travel Outlook](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/fly/flight-information/daily-travel-outlook.html).

If you hold a United ticket, confirm any window on your own reservation or [United's travel alerts page](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) before you pay a change fee.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the airline app or manage booking before you go to the airport.
2. Recheck the [FAA National Airspace System status](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) and your airport's FAA page if you want a ground-stop view that may not show in the app yet.
3. Look for a travel waiver in manage booking before you pay a change fee. If you qualify and your plans are flexible, move while seats remain. American's listed change deadline is September 27th.
4. If the airline canceled your flight, stay on its rebooking path first. Keep receipts for meals or hotels if you get stuck overnight.

Weather delays in the US generally do not trigger a standalone airline cash payment for a simple delay. Under [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds), a cancellation or significant delay is a different conversation if you choose not to travel. If you paid with a card that includes flight-delay insurance, that coverage can still matter for hotel and meal costs after a long enough delay. For a practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

A coastal storm is still producing wind and flood warnings through Sunday, and several airlines still have fee-free change tools for eligible Saturday and Sunday Northeast tickets. Saturday morning FAA boards at Newark, LaGuardia, Boston, and Philadelphia showed only light delays. Check your flight anyway, and use a waiver in manage booking before you pay change fees. American's listed change deadline is September 27th. Delta and JetBlue run through September 30th.
