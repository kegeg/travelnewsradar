United is blocking unauthorized third-party AI assistants from booking, changing, or managing its tickets, including through travel agencies and booking platforms. Tickets you bought yourself are unchanged; if an AI tool booked you before November 11th, 2026, confirm with United that the reservation is ticketed.

Another airline pushed travelers toward its own channels recently too: [Air Canada added a CA$30 phone booking fee](/news/air-canada-phone-booking-fee/) for bookings made with an agent instead of online.

## What United changed

On November 11th, United [said](https://united.mediaroom.com/news-releases?item=125496) it is restricting unauthorized personal AI agents on united.com and the United app, and through travel agencies and aggregators. The same day it revised its [contract of carriage](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/contract-of-carriage.html) and [website terms](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/website-terms-conditions-legal-notices.html).

The contract now lists using an AI agent to make, change, or service a ticket, or to buy any United product, as a prohibited practice. That covers AI assistants in search, messaging, and social apps, AI-enabled browsers, and tools that reach United through an agency. Watching the software, or clicking approve, still counts as using an AI agent.

United also says it can cancel reservations that an AI agent made, changed, or serviced, without notice. Its [Digital Trust Center FAQ](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/help-center/digital-trust-center/artificial-intelligence/third-party-ai-agents.html) says agent bookings made before November 11th, 2026, will not be canceled. The revised [contract of carriage](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/contract-of-carriage.html) still lists reservations made, changed, or serviced by AI agents among those United may cancel without notice, with no date cutoff. If an AI tool already booked you, confirm with United that the reservation is ticketed.

## What still works

You can still buy on [united.com](https://www.united.com/) or in the United app yourself. A human travel agent can still book United. Assistive technology for customers with disabilities is not part of this restriction. United's website terms also allow a feature that only turns your own voice or text into a search that you run and review.

United's FAQ says you can use AI to research a trip if the tool does not access united.com or the app. Then search and buy yourself. United says it does not work with any third-party AI agents yet.

Keep MileagePlus numbers, passwords, and confirmation numbers out of AI tools. If you already shared them, change your password.

## What this means for travelers

If you book United the usual way, nothing material should change. The rule is about who may complete the purchase.

If you have been letting a chatbot, a browser assistant, or another bot finish a United purchase, complete it yourself instead. United may block that booking, and the contract lets it cancel an AI-handled reservation. The same limit applies to seats, upgrades, and other add-ons.

If a travel agency booked you, ask whether a person completed it. Agencies cannot use a third-party AI agent to search, book, or manage United travel unless United has given permission.

United also warns against letting an AI tool check you in. Flight check-in needs a person to acknowledge that bags do not contain banned items such as explosives, and an AI check-in can skip that safety content.

## What to do

1. Make new United bookings on [united.com](https://www.united.com/) or in the app, or with a person.
2. If an AI tool booked a trip before November 11th, confirm with United that the reservation is ticketed.
3. If you shared MileagePlus login details with an AI tool, change that password.

## Bottom line

United now blocks unauthorized third-party AI assistants from booking or managing its tickets. Bookings you made yourself are unchanged. If an AI tool booked you before November 11th, confirm with United that the reservation is ticketed, and complete new purchases yourself on united.com or in the app.
