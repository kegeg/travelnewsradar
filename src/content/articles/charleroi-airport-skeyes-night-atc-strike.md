Skeyes air traffic controllers at Brussels South Charleroi Airport (CRL) are walking out every night from 10 p.m. to 8 a.m. local, from September 16th through October 10th, 2026. Thursday already saw about 40 canceled flights, and Friday morning's first cancellations are already posted.

Another Europe airport labor disruption after [Italy's Sunday airport walkouts](/news/italy-airport-strikes-rome-tuscany-sardinia-venice/). Ryanair is the main airline at Charleroi. Check your airline before you go, including some afternoon trips that cannot get back before 10 p.m.

## What's canceled so far

[Charleroi Airport's September 17th update](https://www.brussels-charleroi-airport.com/fr/action-sociale-chez-skeyes-update) currently lists **12 afternoon and evening departures** and **12 matching arrivals** as canceled today, plus **9 departures** and **9 arrivals** on Friday morning. The airport says those counts can still change.

Nicolas Charluteau, the airport's operations director, put Thursday's total at about **40** arrivals and departures. Some of those are aircraft that cannot leave and return before the 10 p.m. cut, so the trip gets pulled earlier in the day. Wednesday evening, Ryanair canceled **14** round-trip routes, about **28** flights. Everything after 10 p.m. was pulled, and some late-afternoon trips were canceled too so aircraft would not be stuck past the cut. Flying resumes around 8 a.m. when the night shift ends.

[Live departures](https://www.brussels-charleroi-airport.com/en/flights/live) on Thursday afternoon already showed canceled Ryanair trips in that later window. Wizz Air and a few other carriers also fly Charleroi. The walkout is at the Charleroi tower, so [Brussels Airport (BRU)](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en) is a different airport.

## Why controllers are walking out

The dispute is at **Skeyes**, Belgium's air traffic control operator. Charleroi Airport is not a party to it. Controllers at Charleroi want night-shift bonuses brought into line with colleagues at Liège Airport, which runs real overnight flying. Skeyes says Charleroi is a daytime airport, so the night workload is not the same.

If the full 24-night window runs, Skeyes has estimated about **832** flight movements could be hit, including **487** morning flights and **345** night flights. Charleroi Airport puts passengers at up to about **150,000**. Walloon airports minister Cécile Neven has used a figure as high as **300,000**.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold a Charleroi ticket between **10 p.m. and 8 a.m.** local through October 10th, those hours are the walkout window. Afternoon and early-evening flights can also cancel if the return would land after 10 p.m. The first flights after 8 a.m. can cancel if the aircraft did not get back overnight.

A midday flight can still operate if the aircraft is already on the ground at Charleroi. Confirm status anyway, because the posted list moves during the day.

US and Canadian travelers usually reach Charleroi on a short intra-Europe connection, not a transatlantic nonstop. The airport sits about 30 miles (50 km) south of Brussels. A canceled Charleroi flight can still leave you stuck overnight or cost you a day of the trip.

If your ticket is out of Brussels Airport (BRU), this walkout does not apply to you.

## What to do now

1. Check your airline's site, app, and email before you leave for the airport. Charleroi's update and live board give the general picture. Only the airline can rebook you. [Ryanair](https://www.ryanair.com/gb/en) is the carrier most people will need.
2. If the flight is canceled, use the airline's rebooking or refund path first. Under [EU air passenger rules](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), you can choose reimbursement or re-routing, plus assistance (meals, and a hotel if you are stuck overnight). Cash compensation is a separate claim. Airlines often argue that an air traffic control strike is outside their control.
3. Wait for the airline to cancel or rebook the original ticket before you buy a seat on another carrier, or you may lose the refund path.
4. Brussels Airport is only useful if your airline actually moves you there.

## Bottom line

If you fly Charleroi through October 10th, check your airline before you go, including afternoon trips that cannot get back before 10 p.m.
