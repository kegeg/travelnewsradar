Alaska Airlines is still running November's [Atmos Members Day](https://news.alaskaair.com/loyalty/november-atmos-members-day/) award sale through November 7th, 2026. Atmos Rewards members can save up to 50% on award travel to nine cities, with Honolulu, Kahului, Kona, and Lihue starting at 11,500 points one-way.

If you do not have Atmos points, your cash tickets do not change.

## Award cities and dates

Alaska posted the list on November 4th. You have to book by November 7th. Hawaii uses an earlier travel window than the other five cities.

| Destination | One-way starting at | Travel dates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honolulu (HNL), Kahului (OGG), Kona (KOA), Lihue (LIH) | 11,500 points | December 1st, 2026, through March 10th, 2027 |
| Venice (VCE) | 25,000 points | January 15th through May 12th, 2027 |
| Amman (AMM), Catania (CTA), Mumbai (BOM), Singapore (SIN) | 30,000 points | January 15th through May 12th, 2027 |

Those are starting prices. The award search shows the points you pay.

Atmos Members Day is the first Wednesday of every month.

## How to book

Alaska's instructions:

1. Select "use points" when you search on [alaskaair.com](https://www.alaskaair.com/) or [hawaiianairlines.com](https://www.hawaiianairlines.com/).
2. The discounted award fare shows in the results if the date is in the window.
3. Book the flight by November 7th.

You still pay taxes and airline fees in cash. Alaska did not publish a time of day on November 7th.

## What this means for travelers

If you already hold Atmos points and you can travel to Hawaii between December 1st and March 10th, 11,500 points one-way is the number to search. A round trip is two one-way awards when both dates sit in the window.

The starting price is not on every date. Search from your own airport.

Venice at 25,000 points, and Amman, Catania, Mumbai, or Singapore at 30,000, use the January 15th through May 12th, 2027 window.

If you do not already have the points, skip this sale. Canadian credit cards no longer earn Atmos points the easy way. For how the currency still works on partner flights, see [Flytrippers' airline points overview](https://flytrippers.com/airline-points/).

I collect Atmos points. Last year I used 12,500 Atmos points plus about US$80 for a last-minute LATAM hop into Paraguay when cash fares were ugly. I have been to Waikiki. I am not sitting on a Members Day booking tonight.

## What to do

1. If you have Atmos points and dates that fit, log in, check "use points," and book by November 7th.
2. Search your own origin and both directions.
3. If you do not have Atmos points, you can skip this.

## Bottom line

Alaska is still selling November's Atmos Members Day awards through November 7th. Hawaii starts at 11,500 points one-way for travel December 1st through March 10th. Search the dates before you book.
