[Gateside by TSA PreCheck](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/gateside-tsa-precheck) lets eligible TSA PreCheck members and other Known Traveler Number (KTN) holders go past security at **13 US airports** without a boarding pass. The pass is free. You apply online **one to three days** before you visit, and if you are approved you use a PreCheck lane with an [acceptable ID](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/identification).

## How Gateside works

TSA [announced the launch](https://www.tsa.gov/news/press/releases/2026/09/09/tsa-launches-gateside-tsa-precheck-bill-and-hillary-clinton-national) on **Wednesday, September 9th, 2026**. Gateside is a one-day authorization for non-travelers who already qualify for PreCheck screening. TSA lists send-offs, arrivals, a meal during someone else's layover, airport dining and shopping, and greeting returning service members as the intended uses.

Apply at [TSA.gov/gateside](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/gateside-tsa-precheck). TSA says you create or log in to a **login.gov** account, enter personal details that must match your PreCheck record (name, date of birth, and KTN), pick the airport, then check the portal dashboard for approval. You have to apply **at least one day before** the visit and **no more than three days** in advance.

If you are approved, bring an acceptable ID to a **TSA PreCheck lane**. TSA says you may need to log in on your phone and show the officer the live portal status. Screenshots and printouts are not accepted. Children can come if you add them on the same registration; you will need that live portal view so the officer can see everyone at the checkpoint is on it.

The pass is valid for **one calendar day**, with re-entry allowed that day. You cannot use TSA PreCheck Touchless ID lanes. If the PreCheck lane is closed, an officer may send you to a standard lane. Visitors go through the same screening rules as ticketed passengers.

TSA also still lets airlines issue a limited **gate pass** (for example accompanying a minor, a person with disabilities, or a military member). Those people use standard screening lanes, even if they have PreCheck.

Each airport sets a daily cap. TSA says a denial can mean the day's limit is already used, even if your details match. Availability can also be limited at peak times.

## What this means for travelers

If you do not have PreCheck or another KTN, or you are not going to one of these 13 airports as a visitor, this does not apply to you. Ticketed passengers keep using the checkpoint the way they already do.

If you **do** have a KTN and you want to walk someone to a gate or meet someone during a long layover, Gateside is the PreCheck-lane path at those airports. TSA says it is also open to people who access PreCheck screening through **certain federal employee programs** and **other trusted traveler programs**. If you already use PreCheck because you have Global Entry or NEXUS, the number TSA wants is that KTN (for those CBP programs, the PASS ID). You still have to apply and get approved; there is no same-day walk-up.

A regular family member without a KTN still cannot go through with you unless they are a child you added on the registration. Airline gate passes remain the narrow option for some of those cases, through standard lanes.

Caroline's family is in Michigan, and **Detroit Metro (DTW)** is on the first-wave list. We have flown DTW visiting them. If we wanted to walk someone to a gate there, this is the airport where that would actually be possible.

Bring a REAL ID or another document on TSA's acceptable-ID list. If you arrive without one, [TSA ConfirmID](https://www.tsa.gov/tsaconfirm-id/about-confirmid) is the **$45** fallback.

## What to do

Most travelers can ignore this. If you want to use Gateside:

1. Confirm your airport is on the [live list](https://www.tsa.gov/precheck/gateside-tsa-precheck), then apply **one to three days** ahead at TSA.gov/gateside with a KTN that matches your PreCheck record.
2. Check the portal before you leave for the airport. Bring acceptable ID, and be ready to show the live approval on your phone, especially if children are on the reservation.
3. Go to a PreCheck lane, not Touchless ID. If TSA denies the pass because that airport already hit its cap, apply for another date.

## Bottom line

Gateside is a free, one-day TSA PreCheck visitor pass at 13 US airports, launched September 9th, 2026. You apply in advance with a KTN, use a PreCheck lane, and still go through screening. If you are not in that group, nothing at the checkpoint changes.
