Delta still lists its first Atlanta-Riyadh nonstop from **October 23rd, 2026**. After two attacks at King Khalid International Airport (RUH) on Thursday killed three Saudi citizens, including a Saudia captain, CEO Ed Bastian said Friday that safety will decide whether that launch still happens, and that Delta will tell customers soon.

Another Middle East airport story after Wednesday's [Houthi attack on Aden airport](/news/aden-airport-attack-cairo-flight-diverted-jeddah/).

## What Saudi aviation officials confirmed

Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Civil Aviation, in a [Friday statement](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2697151), said King Khalid International Airport was hit by two attacks on **Thursday, October 8th, 2026**. The first targeted airport facilities, and the second targeted a Saudia aircraft. Three Saudi citizens were killed, and other Saudi citizens and residents of various nationalities were injured, from minor to critical. GACA said airport operations have resumed and air traffic has returned to normal.

Saudia said Captain Hamoud Ali al-Kalthami died in the Thursday incident, and that one of its aircraft was damaged on the ground with no passengers aboard.

Yemen's Houthis claimed the Thursday strikes. GACA [earlier reported](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2695853) separate hits at Abha International Airport and King Khalid on October 6th and 7th, with three people killed in those two attacks. The [GCC secretary general](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2696028) attributed those earlier airport attacks to the Houthis.

Yemen has been at war since 2014. The Houthis control much of the country's northwest and have been firing missiles and drones at Saudi airports, energy sites, and shipping. Riyadh is the capital. King Khalid is where Delta planned to land its new Atlanta flight.

## What Delta has published

Delta [announced Atlanta-Riyadh](https://news.delta.com/delta-begin-first-nonstop-flight-riyadh-saudi-arabia) as its first service to Saudi Arabia and the first US-airline nonstop between the United States and Riyadh. An [August follow-up](https://news.delta.com/kingdom-calling-deltas-new-atlanta-riyadh-service-begins-oct-23) still says the route starts October 23rd on an Airbus A350-900, daily through October 30th, then three times a week. Delta's newsroom has not posted a delay or cancellation.

Bastian's Friday comments leave the October 23rd start under review. The route stays on the schedule until Delta changes it.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold a Delta ticket on Atlanta-Riyadh from October 23rd, the flight is still on the published schedule. Wait for Delta's notice before you buy another ticket. If the airline postpones or drops the route, use Delta's rebooking path first.

If you are flying into or out of Riyadh this week on another airline, GACA says the airport is operating again. According to reports, Lufthansa Group has paused Riyadh flights through October 16th, and Indian carriers including IndiGo, Air India, and Akasa Air have paused through roughly October 9th-10th. Check your own booking before you leave for the airport.

If you are already in Riyadh, follow local instructions at the airport. If you are still deciding whether to go, the UK now advises against all but essential travel to Riyadh province. Canada still advises against non-essential travel to the country as a whole, and the US remains at Level 3, Reconsider Travel.

If you do not hold a Riyadh ticket, Thursday's attacks do not change an Atlanta connection on their own.

[Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/saudi-arabia) last updated its page on **October 8th, 2026**. It also tells Canadians to avoid all travel in Asir, Jazan, and Najran, plus within 30 km (about 19 miles) of the Yemen border. The [US advisory](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) was issued March 13th, 2026, with Level 4, Do Not Travel, along the Yemen border. The [UK FCDO page](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/saudi-arabia), still current October 9th, 2026, moved Riyadh province into all-but-essential travel in an October 8th update.

## What to do now

1. If you have booked Delta Atlanta-Riyadh, open the reservation on [delta.com](https://www.delta.com/) or the app and watch for an email or app notice. Use Delta's path if the airline changes the flight.
2. If you are flying Riyadh in the next few days on any airline, confirm the flight status before you go to the airport.
3. Recheck the [Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/saudi-arabia), [US](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html), and [UK](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/saudi-arabia) pages the same day you travel.

## Bottom line

Delta's Atlanta-Riyadh nonstop is still listed from October 23rd. Thursday's attacks at King Khalid airport killed three people, including a Saudia captain, and GACA says traffic is back to normal. Bastian said safety will decide the launch. Open the booking and wait for Delta's notice.
