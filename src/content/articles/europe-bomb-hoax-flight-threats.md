Bomb threats hit passenger flights across Europe on **Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026**, including a LOT diversion into Warsaw Modlin. Officials searched or diverted at least seven aircraft and found no explosives, so if you were on one of those trips, start with the airline.

Another Europe airport disruption after [Italy's airport walkouts at Rome](/news/italy-airport-strikes-rome-tuscany-sardinia-venice/) and [France's CDG and Marseille flight cuts](/news/paris-cdg-marseille-flight-cuts/).

## Which flights were hit

Ryanair had four of the Tuesday flights. LOT, Wizz Air, and SAS were also searched. The UK government called the warnings "malicious hoax threats."

| Flight | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| LOT Heathrow to Warsaw Chopin | Diverted to Modlin; 181 people on board; airport closed, then reopened |
| SAS Copenhagen to Warsaw Chopin | Landed as planned and searched |
| Ryanair Manchester to Murcia | 114 passengers and seven crew searched on the ground |
| Ryanair Lisbon to Rome Fiumicino | Searched after a morning landing |
| Ryanair Dublin to Rome Fiumicino | Italian Air Force escort; searched after landing |
| Wizz Air Bergamo to Sofia | Landed as planned; 176 passengers; searched |
| Ryanair Dublin to Sofia | Landed as planned; 185 passengers; searched |

[Warsaw Modlin Airport](https://modlinairport.pl/aktualnosc/odwolanie-alarmu-bombowego-i-przywrocenie-normalnego-funkcjonowania-lotniska-komunikat-nr) said the Heathrow-Warsaw alert was issued at 9:54 a.m. local and the Boeing 737 MAX landed at 10:01 a.m. Poland is six hours ahead of Eastern Time on Tuesday. The airport closed, resumed flying at 11:50 a.m., and canceled the bomb alert at 12:21 p.m. LOT said no suspicious items were found and passengers went through border checks, then boarded a coach to Chopin.

Modlin also listed knock-on delays: an Edinburgh arrival planned for 11:30 a.m. landed at 12:49 p.m. after a stop in Poznan, and a Tenerife departure planned for 11:45 a.m. left at 12:37 p.m.

Spanish officials said the Manchester-Murcia Ryanair flight landed around 10:30 a.m. local. Guardia Civil bomb technicians found no explosives, and the airport went back to normal operations.

The Italian Air Force escorted the Dublin-Rome Ryanair flight until landing. Fiumicino searched that aircraft and the earlier Lisbon arrival. Nothing was found.

[Bulgaria's transport minister, Georgi Peev](https://bnrnews.bg/en/post/552739/updated-sofia-bound-planes-from-bergamo-and-dublin-searched-after-threats), said the Bergamo and Dublin flights into Sofia were not diverted. Both were searched on arrival. Bulgarian border police said they received an email around 10:35 a.m. local about a threat on the Bergamo flight, and a similar warning against an aircraft arriving from Dublin.

On Monday, Wizz Air said its London Luton-Sofia flight was diverted to Budapest after a bomb threat during the flight. A Hungarian fighter escorted it in. Wizz Air said checks found nothing, and the aircraft later continued to Sofia.

UK Counter-Terrorism Policing said it has no active investigation into these incidents. Authorities have not identified who sent the warnings, and they have not confirmed the cases are linked.

## What this means for travelers

If you were booked on one of the Tuesday flights above, or on Monday's Luton-Sofia Wizz Air hop, your trip already moved: a diversion, a long search on the ground, a coach from Modlin to Chopin, or a missed connection. Check what the airline assigned next.

If you were booked through Modlin on Tuesday morning, the closure delayed other flying there, including that Edinburgh arrival and Tenerife departure. Confirm your own times.

If you fly from or to those airports later, operations resumed after the searches.

I've flown that Luton to Sofia Wizz Air hop.

## What to do

1. Check **your flight** in the airline app or manage booking. [Ryanair](https://www.ryanair.com/), [Wizz Air](https://www.wizzair.com/), [LOT](https://www.lot.com/), and [SAS](https://www.flysas.com/) all had aircraft searched.
2. If the airline diverted, delayed, or canceled your flight, use its rebooking or refund path first, including any coach or later flight it assigns. Give that path a chance before you buy a seat on another carrier.
3. Where [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm) apply, a long delay can mean meals and, if you are stuck overnight, a hotel. Cash compensation is a separate claim and is case-specific. A bomb threat can count as an extraordinary circumstance if the airline proves the required link.

Keep receipts. Card flight-delay insurance on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the delay meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Officials searched or diverted at least seven passenger flights across Europe on Tuesday after bomb threats, and found no explosives. If you were on one of those trips, use the airline's rebooking path.
