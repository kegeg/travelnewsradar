Delta restored its weight-and-balance system Sunday, November 22nd, 2026, after a technology outage delayed more than 1,000 flights, mostly at its Atlanta hub. The airline called operations stable Monday. Check your flight if you were booked Sunday or still connect through Atlanta this week.

The FAA said these delays were not related to its air traffic systems, more than a week after [Friday's nationwide ground stop](/news/faa-nationwide-ground-stop/). [United had its own departing-flight tech issue](/news/united-tech-outage-delayed-departures/) last Monday.

## What happened

Delta said Sunday morning that teams were using manual workarounds so flights could leave while it restored the automation that calculates how heavy an airplane is and how that weight is distributed. Crews need those figures before they can push back. Doing the math by hand takes longer.

The FAA held some Atlanta-bound flights on the ground for about an hour Sunday morning, then used a ground delay program into Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport. "These delays are not related to any FAA air traffic systems," the agency said. "Please check with Delta for the latest information."

Delta said later Sunday afternoon that the system was working again, and that the outage was not a computer hack. It also said about 49% of its flights Sunday were delayed by more than 15 minutes. Most of those delays were at Atlanta, and the same equipment issue delayed flights heading to Atlanta from other airports.

FlightAware counted about 140 cancellations Sunday. Delta said about 70% of the delays and cancellations on that tracker were tied to the automation issue, and the rest to weather and air traffic programs in the Northeast.

"Delta is rebooking customers to the next best itinerary and providing meals and hotel rooms where necessary," the airline said. "For customers facing a canceled flight and don't wish to travel, refunds are being given."

On Monday, Delta said operations were stable, with automation for departing-flight weight and balance calculations fully restored.

As of about 10:50 p.m. Eastern Monday, the [FAA status board](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) was not listing a ground stop or delay program at Atlanta. The [FAA's Atlanta airport page](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=ATL&p=1) showed gate-hold, taxi, and arrival delays of 15 minutes or less.

Delta's [current advisories page](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/current-advisories), checked Monday evening, did not show a dedicated change window for this Sunday outage. The Air Traffic Control Outage waiver still listed there is for [Friday's FAA stop](/news/faa-ground-stop-recovery-airline-waivers/), with original travel November 13th through 16th.

## What this means for travelers

If you had a Delta flight Sunday, check **your itinerary**. Delta said it was rebooking people onto the next best option. A delayed airplane or crew can still miss a later connection even after the system came back.

If you fly to, from, or through Atlanta later this week for Thanksgiving, the automation is restored. Aircraft and crews can still be in the wrong city, so check your flight.

If you are still booking a trip after this week, nothing material should change.

Canadian itineraries that connect in Atlanta take the same hit. A Canada departure can still be late if the onward airplane sat at an Atlanta gate Sunday.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the [Fly Delta app or on delta.com](https://www.delta.com/) before you go to the airport.
2. If Delta delayed or canceled your flight, use [rebooking options](https://www.delta.com/us/en/change-cancel/delayed-or-canceled-flight) in the app or My Trips first. Delta says it will try to rebook you automatically. You can search for alternative flights there, or cancel a rebooked flight and request a refund of the unflown portion.
3. If Delta canceled your flight, or delayed a domestic itinerary by 3 hours or more (6 hours international) and you choose not to travel, [Delta's delayed-flight refund path](https://www.delta.com/us/en/change-cancel/delayed-or-canceled-flight) and [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds) apply. Keep receipts if you get stuck overnight.

Delta also says US and Canada residents can request meal, hotel, and hotel-transfer reimbursement when a cancellation or a delay of 3 hours or more is within its control. A short delay that you still fly generally does not trigger a standalone cash payment. Card flight-delay insurance can still cover a hotel or meals after a long enough wait. For a checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Delta restored its weight-and-balance automation Sunday afternoon after more than 1,000 delays, mostly at Atlanta. The airline called Monday stable. Check your flight if you were booked Sunday or connect through Atlanta this week. Delta's current advisories page had no dedicated waiver for this outage.
