Brussels Airport (BRU) expects **around 140** flight cancellations on **Friday, October 9th, 2026**, after staff at ground handler **Alyzia** are expected to join a national demonstration. The airport currently says **more than half** of Alyzia-handled flights should still operate as planned. If you have booked into or out of BRU on Friday, check your airline before you leave for the airport.

Another Belgium airport labor story after [Charleroi's night controller strike](/news/charleroi-airport-skeyes-night-atc-strike/). That walkout is at Brussels South Charleroi, a different airport.

## What's happening Friday

[Brussels Airport](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passengers/contact/airlines) says a national demonstration is planned Friday, and part of Alyzia's workforce is expected to take part. Alyzia is a ground-handling company that loads bags, turns aircraft, and moves luggage between the plane and the terminal for the airlines that hire it. More than half of the flights it handles should still go; the rest are expected to cancel.

Airlines that use Alyzia are contacting affected passengers directly. The airport's flight board will show a cancellation only after the airline files it.

[Brussels Airlines](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/plan-your-trip/flight-information), the airport's largest airline and an Alyzia client, says the expected shortage at Alyzia means it has to cancel part of Friday's schedule. Spokesperson Nico Cardone put that cut at about **40%**. The airline had expected to carry around **30,000** passengers on Friday. It says it will email customers whose flights are pulled, with an alternative or a refund, and that you can also check [My Bookings](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/my-bookings/retrieve-booking.html). Brussels Airlines says it is outside this dispute.

## Who is affected

Alyzia handles flights for around **20** airlines at Brussels Airport. Those Friday tickets are the ones that can cancel.

Aviapartner is the airport's other passenger ground handler. The airport currently expects **no impact** on Aviapartner-handled flights. Use the airport's [airline and handler list](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passengers/contact/airlines) to see which company works your flight, and confirm with the airline.

A separate public-sector union action is planned for **Monday, October 12th**. Brussels Airport currently expects flights to operate that day. It does expect public-transport disruption, and tells travelers to check [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/), [STIB/MIVB](https://www.stib-mivb.be/), and [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/) before heading to or from the airport.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked **on Brussels Airlines** into or out of BRU on Friday, you are the largest group that can get hit. About 40% of that airline's Friday flying is expected to cancel. Check the reservation either way.

If you have booked on another airline at BRU, check who handles the flight. Alyzia clients can see cancellations. Aviapartner clients are currently expected to operate. Many US and Canadian trips use Brussels Airlines or another long-haul into BRU, then a same-day Europe hop. The BRU segment is the one to check for Friday.

If your Friday flight still operates, plan to travel as booked and confirm status before you leave. Some cancellations will only appear on the airport board once the airline registers them. If you fly Saturday or later, Friday's demonstration is already over.

## What to do now

1. Check **your airline** first. For Brussels Airlines, use [current information](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/plan-your-trip/flight-information) and [My Bookings](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/my-bookings/retrieve-booking.html). For other carriers, use that airline's app or manage-booking page. [Brussels Airport](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en) has a flight search on the homepage.
2. If the airline cancels your flights, use its rebooking or refund path first, including any alternative it emails you. If you buy a seat on another carrier before following that process, the replacement fare may not be reimbursed.
3. For flights covered by [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), you can choose reimbursement or re-routing, plus care (meals, and a hotel if you are stuck overnight). Cash compensation is a separate claim. Airlines often argue that a third-party strike is outside their control.

Keep receipts if you are stranded. Trip-cancellation or interruption coverage on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the cancellation meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Brussels Airport expects around 140 cancellations on Friday as Alyzia ground handlers are expected to join a national demonstration. More than half of Alyzia-handled flights should still operate. Brussels Airlines is cutting about 40% of its Friday schedule. Check your airline before you go.
