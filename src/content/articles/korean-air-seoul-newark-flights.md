Korean Air [said on November 12th, 2026](https://news.koreanair.com/%eb%8c%80%ed%95%9c%ed%95%ad%ea%b3%b5-%ec%9d%b8%ec%b2%9c%eb%89%b4%ec%96%b4%ed%81%ac-%eb%85%b8%ec%84%a0-%ec%8b%a0%ea%b7%9c-%ec%b7%a8%ed%95%ad-%eb%82%b4%eb%85%84-5%ec%9b%94-22%ec%9d%bc%eb%b6%80/) it will start **daily** flights between Seoul Incheon (ICN) and Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) on **May 22nd, 2027**. The route uses a Boeing 777-300ER. Korean Air already flies Incheon-JFK.

Another 2027 schedule add after [American's summer 2027 international routes](/news/american-airlines-summer-2027-international-routes/). This one is Korea to Newark.

## The new flights

| Flight | Route | Departs | Arrives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KE097 | Incheon (ICN) to Newark (EWR) | 10:00 p.m. | 11:00 p.m. |
| KE098 | Newark (EWR) to Incheon (ICN) | 1:00 a.m. | 5:15 a.m. next day |

Times are as Korean Air published them, local at each airport, every day. Recheck [koreanair.com](https://www.koreanair.com/) for your travel day; schedules this far out can still move. Korean Air has not published an end date.

[koreanair.com already lists Seoul-Newark fares](https://www.koreanair.com/flights/en/flights-from-seoul-to-newark), including dates before May 22nd, 2027. Check that the operating flight is Korean Air's new nonstop before you lock hotels or an onward ticket around a specific departure.

## What this means for travelers

If you want a **Korean Air nonstop between Seoul Incheon and Newark** from May 22nd, 2027, there is now a published start date, aircraft, flight numbers, and timetable. Nothing about US or South Korean entry rules changes because of this schedule add. You still need the right documents for your nationality.

Korean Air already sells [Incheon-JFK](https://www.koreanair.com/flights/en/flights-from-seoul-to-new-york). Newark and JFK are different airports. Newark can be the easier New York-area pick if you are going to New Jersey or connecting there. JFK can still be simpler if your hotel or onward flight is built around that airport. Price the full trip, including ground time, not just the new nonstop segment.

United already sells Newark-Seoul. It [announced daily Newark-Incheon](https://www.united.com/en/us/newsroom/announcements/cision-125413) from **September 4th, 2026**, and [united.com still lists EWR-ICN fares](https://www.united.com/en/us/deals/flights-from-new-york-to-seoul) into 2027. [Air Premia currently operates the same airport pair](https://www.airpremia.com/a/en/customer/notice/schedule/806), with a published winter timetable through **March 28th, 2027**.

Korean Air says the new flights are part of its joint venture with Delta, so customers of both airlines can connect on one itinerary when the ticket is issued that way.

## Bottom line

Korean Air plans daily Incheon-Newark flights from May 22nd, 2027 on a Boeing 777-300ER, as KE097 and KE098. It already flies Incheon-JFK. Search [koreanair.com](https://www.koreanair.com/) and confirm the operating nonstop before you lock hotels or onward tickets around a start date.
