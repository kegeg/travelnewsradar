Statistics Canada counted **1.9 million** Canadian-resident return trips from the United States by car and air in October, [up 3.0%](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261112/dq261112c-eng.htm) from October 2025. It is the seventh straight month of year-over-year growth. Adding the two modes Statistics Canada published, the total is still about **27%** below October 2024.

If you already have a US trip booked, Thursday's release does not change your ticket. [Air Transat's current winter schedule filings drop Montreal and Halifax Fort Lauderdale](/news/air-transat-winter-us-fort-lauderdale-cuts/). The airline has not explained the change.

## The October numbers

The [leading indicator](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261112/dq261112c-eng.htm) is preliminary. It covers automobile and commercial air arrivals only. Complete Frontier Counts come with "Travel between Canada and other countries" on **December 21st, 2026**.

| Mode | October 2026 | vs October 2025 | vs October 2024 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automobile | 1,419,000 | **+2.2%** | **-29.0%** |
| Air | 461,915 | **+5.6%** | **-19.6%** |
| Combined | 1.9 million | **+3.0%** | about **-27%** |

Air trips grew faster than car trips versus 2025. Car trips are still the larger hole versus 2024, and they are still most of the volume.

September's gain from the [previous monthly release](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/261013/dq261013a-eng.htm) was **6.8%**. October's **3.0%** is a smaller year-over-year bump.

From August 2026, Statistics Canada is gradually adding **NEXUS Highway** travelers to this indicator. The agency tells users to be careful comparing August onward with earlier years. Some of the year-over-year increase can be extra coverage as well as extra trips.

## What 2025 already changed

A [Statistics Canada review of 2025](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/36-28-0001/2026007/article/00005-eng.htm) put the full-year drop in Canadian-resident return crossings from the United States at **25.4%**. Leisure visits fell **21.5%**. Visits to see friends and relatives fell **9.0%**.

That is the base October is growing off. Canadians did not stop going south.

US-resident trips **to** Canada by car and air reached **1.5 million** in October, up **8.1%** from a year earlier. That is the ninth straight month of year-over-year growth on that side of the border. Canadian-resident return trips from overseas by air were **1.0 million**, up **0.5%**.

## What this means for travelers

Nothing material should change for a trip you already hold. Thursday's release announces no new passport, NEXUS, or CBP rule.

If you are still deciding on a winter US trip, confirm the airline still operates your city pair. Car trips are still **29%** below October 2024. The [Gordie Howe Bridge](/news/gordie-howe-bridge-opens-windsor-detroit/) is an extra crossing if you are driving that corridor.

Caroline is from Michigan, and we were there in August and September for family. Those trips still happen. In 2025, family visits fell less than leisure.

## What to do

1. If you are already booked, there is nothing to rebook from this data drop.
2. If you are still choosing a winter US flight, confirm the airline still operates your city pair before you pay.
3. If you are driving Windsor-Detroit, check live waits and pick Ambassador, the tunnel, or Gordie Howe on the day.

## Bottom line

October's 3% rise left Canadian return trips from the US about **27%** below October 2024. Car trips are still down **29%** from two years ago; air trips are down **19.6%**. This release does not change existing bookings.
