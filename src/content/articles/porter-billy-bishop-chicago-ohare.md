Porter [started twice-daily flights](https://www.flyporter.com/en-us/about-porter/media-centre/news-details?title=Porter+Airlines+launches+Chicago-O%27Hare+flights+from+Toronto%27s+Billy+Bishop+2026+10) between Toronto's downtown Billy Bishop airport (YTZ) and Chicago O'Hare (ORD), the airline said on **October 26th, 2026**. The route uses a 78-seat De Havilland Dash 8-400. You clear US customs and immigration in Toronto and arrive at O'Hare as a domestic passenger.

This is a different Porter story than the [VIPorter fuel surcharge](/news/porter-viporter-fuel-surcharge/). This one is a route add.

## The new flights

| Route | Departs | Arrives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Billy Bishop (YTZ) to O'Hare (ORD) | 10:10 a.m. / 4:35 p.m. | 11:05 a.m. / 5:30 p.m. |
| O'Hare (ORD) to Billy Bishop (YTZ) | 11:45 a.m. / 6:10 p.m. | 2:25 p.m. / 8:50 p.m. |

Times are as Porter published them, local at each airport. Recheck [flyporter.com](https://www.flyporter.com) for your travel day.

Porter has served Chicago from Billy Bishop since 2008, previously at Midway. Its [Chicago flights page](https://www.flyporter.com/en_us/flights-from-chicago) says that after **October 24th** it ends Midway (MDW) flying and moves all Chicago service to O'Hare. If you were used to Porter on Chicago's southwest side, this is a different airport.

The flights leave from downtown Billy Bishop, not Toronto Pearson.

## Preclearance at Billy Bishop

Billy Bishop's US CBP preclearance facility [opened March 10th, 2026](https://www.billybishopairport.com/flights/check-in-security/preclearance/), months before this route started. Every US-bound passenger at YTZ goes through it after airline check-in and Canadian security.

You complete US immigration, customs, and agriculture inspection in Toronto, then skip those lines on arrival and can connect like a domestic passenger. [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/preclearance) lists Billy Bishop preclearance hours as **6 a.m. to 8 p.m.** Porter's published YTZ departures sit inside that window.

The airport says a **$22.50 CAD** CBP user fee applies per US-bound passenger in the first year. Airlines collect it when you buy the ticket. Bring a valid passport. Non-Canadian citizens may also need a visa or ESTA.

## Connections through O'Hare

Porter says you can connect on American Airlines through O'Hare, and names Denver, Houston, Minneapolis, Orange County, San Diego, and Seattle as examples. Price the full trip, and check that the connection time and terminals work on your specific booking.

## What this means for travelers

If you want a **downtown Toronto-Chicago nonstop**, Porter now sells Billy Bishop-O'Hare twice a day. Nothing about US or Canadian entry rules changes because of this schedule add. You still need the right documents, and you still preclear at YTZ.

O'Hare is Chicago's main connecting airport. That only helps if your onward flight is actually timed to these two daily departures.

Search the dates on [flyporter.com](https://www.flyporter.com) before you lock hotels or an onward ticket around a specific departure.

## Bottom line

Porter is now flying twice daily between Billy Bishop and Chicago O'Hare on a 78-seat Dash 8-400, with US preclearance in Toronto. Midway service has ended. Search [flyporter.com](https://www.flyporter.com) for your dates.
