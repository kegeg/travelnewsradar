United said it resolved a technology issue Monday afternoon, November 16th, 2026, that delayed some departing flights, with no impact on flights already in the air or on United Express. Check your flight in the United app if you were booked on a mainline departure Monday evening.

The FAA said this was a United technology issue, three days after [Friday's nationwide ground stop](/news/faa-nationwide-ground-stop/).

## What happened

United told customers Monday afternoon it was dealing with a "widespread system outage" affecting departing flights, then that it was troubleshooting a technology issue. Around 6 p.m. Eastern it said the issue was resolved.

"We have resolved a technology issue that delayed flight departures," United said. "While some flights were delayed, there was no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights."

United also said there is no indication the outage was a cybersecurity issue.

United's pilots union told members the outage was affecting multiple applications used by crews and that the airline could not provide outbound flights with final weights. Those figures, based on the actual passengers, bags, cargo, and fuel on board, are what crews use before they push back. United itself called it a technology issue and did not publish a cause.

As of about 8:20 p.m. Eastern Monday, the [FAA status board](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) was not listing a nationwide ground stop. San Francisco was showing an average delay of 98 minutes for low ceilings. That weather delay is separate.

United's [travel alerts page](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) Monday evening did not show a dedicated window for this outage. The same page still says United has dropped most change fees. Open manage booking before you pay a fare difference.

Airlines spent the weekend [working through Friday's FAA stop](/news/faa-ground-stop-recovery-airline-waivers/).

## What this means for travelers

If you had a United mainline departure Monday afternoon or evening, check **your flight**. United says the technology issue is resolved. A delayed airplane or crew can still miss a later connection.

United Express, the regional flights operated for United, was not affected, according to the airline. A connection from a United Express flight onto a delayed mainline departure can still be late.

If you are still booking a trip later this week, nothing material should change.

Canadian itineraries on United mainline take the same hit. A Canada departure can still be late if the onward United airplane sat at the gate Monday afternoon.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the [United app or on united.com](https://www.united.com/en/us/flightstatus/) before you go to the airport.
2. If United delayed or canceled your flight, use [rebooking options](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/missed-delayed-or-canceled-flights.html) in the app or website first. You can change, stand by, or cancel and request a refund from there.
3. If United canceled your flight, or delayed a domestic flight by 3 hours or more (6 hours international) and you choose not to travel, [United's refund policy](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/refund-policy.html) and [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds) apply. Keep receipts if you get stuck overnight.

A United technology issue generally does not trigger a standalone cash payment for a short delay if you still fly. Card flight-delay insurance can still cover a hotel or meals after a long enough wait. For a checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

United resolved a Monday afternoon technology issue that delayed some mainline departures. Flights already airborne and United Express were not affected. Check your flight if you were booked Monday evening. United's travel-alerts page had no dedicated waiver for this outage.
