Air Canada plans to restart year-round Toronto Pearson (YYZ) to Tel Aviv (TLV) flights on **November 30th, 2026**, earlier than the mid-January 2027 window it posted in August. The route will run four times a week on Boeing 787-8 and 787-9 aircraft.

This updates [Air Canada’s Dubai and Tel Aviv suspension into 2027](/news/air-canada-dubai-tel-aviv-suspension/), where Dubai stays canceled until mid-January 2027. United already brought Newark-Tel Aviv back. [United’s delayed Dubai restart](/news/united-newark-dubai-restart-delayed-march-2027/) is a different airline and a different ticket.

## What Air Canada changed

On **September 15th, 2026**, Air Canada posted a [Tel Aviv restart note](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/service-to-tel-aviv-resumes.html) and put the same date on its [Middle East travel update](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/middle-east-travel.html). The airline says the move follows a review of the security environment in the region, plus operational and safety considerations.

The August 4th update on that Middle East page had kept both Dubai and Tel Aviv canceled until mid-January 2027. The new note pulls Tel Aviv forward. Dubai flights remain canceled until mid-January 2027.

Inventory for the November 30th restart is already showing in Air Canada’s Toronto-Tel Aviv booking tool. Recheck [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) for your exact travel day. Schedules can still move.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold an Air Canada ticket that still shows Tel Aviv on a date before November 30th, that flight is still inside the canceled window. Open the booking and wait for Air Canada’s rebooking path before you buy another ticket.

If you were waiting for the mid-January restart, November 30th is now the first date Air Canada is selling for its own Toronto-Tel Aviv service. Recheck the booking before you lock hotels around a single day.

Dubai tickets are unchanged. If your itinerary still shows Dubai on Air Canada this fall or early winter, keep using the airline’s live advisory and rebooking options. Other airlines continue to operate some Dubai flights; confirm the operating carrier before you lock hotels around a connection.

[Global Affairs Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/israel-and-palestine) currently tells Canadians to avoid non-essential travel to Israel, last updated **September 10th, 2026**. The US advisory is **Level 3: Reconsider Travel** on the [State Department Israel, West Bank, and Gaza page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/israel-west-bank-and-gaza-travel-advisory.html). A [US Embassy Jerusalem update](https://il.usembassy.gov/travel-advisory-israel-august-2026/) on **August 26th, 2026** said the Department of State renewed that advisory the same day, with no change to the level. Recheck those pages before you keep a leisure trip on the books.

## Flexible booking options: check the live page

Air Canada still lists a flexible booking policy under the March 17th, 2026 update on the Middle East page for planned travel to or from Dubai and Tel Aviv. As printed there, you can rebook the same destination between September 8th and December 15th, 2026, at no fee on Air Canada-operated flights in the cabin you bought, or change to another Air Canada destination in Europe, the U.K., India, or Africa for travel between May 1st and December 15th, 2026. You can also cancel: Aeroplan members can self-serve an AC Wallet credit, or call the contact center for a Future Travel Credit. Refunds remain subject to fare rules.

Those printed dates are older than the September 15th restart note. November 30th now sits inside the printed Tel Aviv rebook window; Dubai’s mid-January 2027 cancellation still sits after December 15th. Open your booking on [Air Canada Trips](https://www.aircanada.com/home/ca/en/aco/trips) and call the contact center if the self-serve path does not match what you need.

## What to do now

1. Open your reservation and confirm whether Tel Aviv or Dubai is still showing on an Air Canada flight number, and on which date.
2. Read the latest dated note on Air Canada’s [Middle East travel update](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/middle-east-travel.html) before you pay a change fee.
3. Recheck Canada’s [Israel advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/israel-and-palestine) and the [US Level 3 advisory](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/israel-west-bank-and-gaza-travel-advisory.html) before you book or keep a leisure trip.

## Bottom line

Air Canada now plans to restart Toronto-Tel Aviv on November 30th, 2026, four times a week, after keeping the route canceled into mid-January 2027. Dubai stays on the later cancellation. Ticketed travelers should manage the booking against the live advisory.
