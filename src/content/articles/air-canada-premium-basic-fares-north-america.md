As of November 17th, 2026, Air Canada is selling Premium Economy Basic and Business Class Basic on flights within Canada and between Canada and the United States. You get the same premium seat, meal, and priority boarding as Standard, at a lower cash price.

If you are booking economy, this does not apply. Economy already had a Basic fare.

The two brands first appeared in July 2026 on some international routes. Air Canada's [domestic tariff](https://www.aircanada.com/content/dam/aircanada/portal/documents/PDF/en/ac_domestic_tariff_en.pdf) already listed them for tickets issued on or after July 27th, 2026. They now show up on Canada and US searches. Air Canada has not published a newsroom release.

## What the Basic fares include

Air Canada's [fare-brand table](https://air-canada.eu/introducing-premium-basic-fares-2/) and the tariff agree on the core rules. Confirm the live fare card on [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) before you pay, because the public [optional services](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/legal/products-and-services.html) page still does not list these Basic premium brands in its change-fee table.

| | Premium Economy Basic | Business Class Basic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refunds | None, and no future travel credit | None, and no future travel credit |
| Changes | Allowed, for a fee | Allowed, for a fee |
| Advance seat selection | For a fee | For a fee |
| Checked bags | 1 | 1 |
| Carry-on | Yes | Yes |
| Lounge (from the fare) | Not included | Maple Leaf Lounge and Air Canada Café; not the Signature Suite |
| Priority boarding and bags | Included | Included |
| Onboard meal | Included | Included |
| Aeroplan points and SQC | Yes | Yes |
| eUpgrades | Not eligible | Already in business class |

Standard in the same cabin still includes a second checked bag and free advance seat selection. It also lets you cancel for a future travel credit, for a fee. Basic does not.

Business Class Basic's Maple Leaf access matters on these North American itineraries. The Signature Suite is an international product, so that exclusion rarely changes a Canada or US trip. If you already have Maple Leaf access through [Aeroplan Elite Status](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/status.html), that status benefit still applies on a Premium Economy Basic ticket.

## Points, SQC, and eUpgrades

On Air Canada-issued tickets, Aeroplan pays [4 Status Qualifying Credits (SQC) per $1](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/status.html) of base fare and carrier surcharges in Premium Economy and Business Class. Taxes, airport fees, and third-party charges do not count. Economy Basic still earns no SQC.

Air Canada also counts Lifetime Qualifying Miles at 125% of distance in Premium Economy (including Basic) and 150% in Business Class (including Basic), on cash fares of Economy Standard or higher.

Members [earn 1 Aeroplan point per $1](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/earn/air-canada.html) on that same base. Elite members earn 2x to 6x depending on status at the time of travel.

[eUpgrades](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/status/eupgrades.html) cannot be used on Economy Basic or Premium Economy Basic. If you planned to buy premium economy and then upgrade into business, Standard is the lowest Premium Economy fare Air Canada still lists as eligible.

Aeroplan flight rewards, including Points + Cash, are a separate path. Reward tickets do not earn points or SQC, and award prices stay on their own chart.

## What this means for travelers

A ticket you already hold in Standard or Latitude keeps the rules you bought.

If you are shopping a premium cabin on a Canada or US itinerary, put Basic and Standard on the same search. The cabin is the same. Basic costs less because it is nonrefundable, includes one checked bag, charges for picking a seat in advance, and is ineligible for eUpgrades.

If your dates might move, or you need that second bag or a chosen seat, Standard can still be the better buy. The gap varies by route and date, so read both fare cards rather than guessing.

Flytrippers' [guide to fare classes versus seat classes](https://flytrippers.com/plane-ticket-fare-classes/) is the useful split if Basic and Standard look interchangeable: they are fares in the same cabin.

## What to do

1. Search [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) and open Basic and Standard in the cabin you want.
2. Skip Basic if you need a refund path, a second checked bag, a free assigned seat, or an eUpgrade.
3. If you redeem Aeroplan, this does not change award tickets.

## Bottom line

Air Canada now sells Basic fares in premium economy and business class on Canada and US flights. You keep the seat and the meal. Refunds, a second bag, free seat selection, and eUpgrades stay with Standard.
