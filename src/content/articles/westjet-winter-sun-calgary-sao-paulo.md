WestJet [published its winter 2026/27 Latin America and Caribbean schedule](https://westjet.mediaroom.com/2026-08-17-Chase-the-rays-this-winter-with-WestJets-biggest-sun-destination-schedule-yet) on **August 17th, 2026**, calling it the airline’s largest sun program yet. Capacity across that network is up **12%** year over year, with up to **1,252** weekly flights on **88** unique nonstop routes. The headline add for Western Canada is nonstop **Calgary–São Paulo**. WestJet says it is the only direct link between Western Canada and South America.

Another winter schedule story after [Air Transat’s Fort Lauderdale winter cuts](/news/air-transat-winter-us-fort-lauderdale-cuts/). This one adds capacity.

## Calgary to São Paulo

WestJet plans **three weekly** Calgary (YYC)–São Paulo Guarulhos (GRU) flights this winter on a Boeing 787 Dreamliner. The block time is about **14 hours**. [WestJet’s South America page](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/best-of-travel/south-america) currently lists the season as **December 2026 through February 2027**, with schedules subject to change. Recheck westjet.com for your exact travel dates before you lock hotels or onward tickets.

The route is also a connection play. Through WestJet’s LATAM partnership, the airline says guests can book **60+** onward South American cities on one WestJet.com itinerary, including places such as Rio de Janeiro, Lima, Santiago, and Buenos Aires. That only helps if the connection times and bag rules on your specific booking work. Price the full trip, not just the new nonstop segment.

## What else is growing in the sun schedule

WestJet’s August 17th release focuses capacity growth on Prairie and Alberta gateways. Highlights it published:

| Market | WestJet’s stated winter change |
| --- | --- |
| Calgary | **18%** more Latin America/Caribbean capacity; twice-daily Los Cabos; more Cancun; new **Mérida** nonstop |
| Edmonton | **34%** more capacity; double last year’s weekly flights to Los Cabos and Montego Bay |
| Saskatoon | **77%** more capacity; Puerto Vallarta and Cancun rise from four to five weekly in peak periods |
| Winnipeg | **26%** more capacity; more Liberia, Cancun, Puerto Vallarta, and Los Cabos; new **Belize City** and **Punta Cana** |
| Regina | **64%** more capacity; first nonstop **Los Cabos** service |

Mérida was already detailed in WestJet’s [July 20th, 2026 new-routes release](https://westjet.mediaroom.com/2026-07-20-From-parkas-to-paradise-WestJet-adds-five-new-routes-for-winter-2026-2027): **weekly** Calgary–Mérida from **December 17th, 2026**. That same package put Regina–Los Cabos and Winnipeg–Belize City from mid-December, Winnipeg–Punta Cana from late November, and Ottawa–Liberia from late December. Those start dates are the current published plan; reconfirm them in the booking tool.

Calgary’s Dreamliner winter mix also returns selected Dreamliner flying to Cancun, Puerto Vallarta, and Maui, alongside year-round Dreamliner service to London Heathrow, Paris, and Tokyo.

## What this means for travelers

If you want a **Western Canada–Brazil nonstop** in peak winter, WestJet now sells Calgary–São Paulo for a short December-to-February window. Nothing about Brazilian entry rules changes because of this schedule add. You still need a valid passport, any required visa or eVisa for your nationality, and enough connection time if São Paulo is only a hub.

If you are shopping sun beaches rather than Brazil, Prairie gateways also pick up more nonstops and higher frequencies into Mexico and the Caribbean. Los Cabos, Cancun, Mérida, Belize City, and Punta Cana all gain new or thicker WestJet flying depending on your home airport.

Schedules this far out can still move by a day or a frequency. Recheck [westjet.com](https://www.westjet.com/) for your exact dates and frequencies before you lock hotels or onward tickets.

## Bottom line

WestJet’s winter 2026/27 sun schedule raises Latin America and Caribbean capacity across multiple Canadian cities. Calgary–São Paulo is the long-haul standout for Western Canada, currently sold three times weekly from December 2026 through February 2027. Search the dates on [westjet.com](https://www.westjet.com/) before you lock hotels or onward tickets around a seasonal start or end date.
