Naples Capodichino Airport (NAP) is closed to flights from November 1st through November 30th, 2026, while airport operator GESAC rebuilds the runway and taxiways. Some routes are operating from Salerno Costa d'Amalfi Airport (QSR) instead. If you hold a Naples ticket in November, check the airline before you go to either airport.

Another Italy airport disruption this year after [Catania's Etna ash closures](/news/catania-airport-etna-ash-ferragosto/).

## What closed

GESAC [said on October 29th, 2026](https://www.aeroportosalerno.it/documents/d/gesac/comunicato-stampa_chiusura-aeroporto-novembre_29-10-26-pdf?download=true), that Naples will stay closed to air traffic for the whole month so crews can strip and rebuild the runway pavement and replace the runway lighting. The work is scheduled around the clock and is budgeted at €30 million. An [English notice](https://www.aeroportodinapoli.it/en/w/airport-closed) went up on September 21st.

## Which flights moved to Salerno

GESAC's [November 1st to 30th Salerno list](https://www.aeroportodinapoli.it/en/w/flights-and-transport-salerno-airport) names 15 destinations. If your city is not on it, the airline may have canceled the flight or reissued the ticket instead of shifting it to Salerno.

| Bergamo | Ryanair |
| Milan Malpensa | easyJet |
| Milan Linate | Aeroitalia, ITA Airways |
| Genoa, Turin, Trieste | Aeroitalia |
| Amsterdam | Transavia |

Both Salerno terminals are open for the extra traffic. Flights to Casablanca, Dubai, Sharm el-Sheikh, and Madrid use the General Aviation Terminal. A free shuttle runs between the two terminals, about 1,300 meters (0.8 miles) apart, from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. local, about every 20 minutes.

The [Salerno destinations page](https://www.aeroportosalerno.it/it/voli-diretti) says the passenger terminal is closed from 10:30 p.m. to 5:00 a.m. except for delayed flights. Recheck your terminal on the booking.

## ITA's Rome link is a bus

[ITA Airways](https://www.ita-airways.com/us/en/book-and-prepare/current-information) is operating Milan Linate flights from Salerno. Rome Fiumicino flights are a replacement bus, not a Salerno flight.

ITA's [bus page](https://www.ita-airways.com/it/en/local-page/bus-napoli) still uses Naples Capodichino as the Naples end:

- AZ1920 leaves Capodichino at 3:50 a.m. Report to the ITA Info Point in Arrivals between 3:00 a.m. and 3:35 a.m.
- AZ1922 leaves at 11:50 a.m. Report between 10:50 a.m. and 11:35 a.m.
- AZ1921 leaves Rome Fiumicino at 9:30 a.m. from Airport Bus Station stall 5. Report between 8:30 a.m. and 9:20 a.m.
- AZ1923 leaves Fiumicino at 4:00 p.m. Report between 3:00 p.m. and 3:50 p.m.

If you land at Fiumicino and continue to Naples on the bus, collect your bags before you go to the stall. ITA tells passengers facing cancellations or schedule changes to call +39 06 85960020 (or the number for your country) about alternatives or a refund where the rules allow it.

## How to get between Naples and Salerno

[Busitalia Campania](https://www.fsbusitalia.it/eng/campania/timetables-and-lines/special-service-napoli-centrale-salerno-airport.html) is running a special bus from November 1st through November 30th between Napoli Centrale (Piazza Garibaldi / Via Galileo Ferraris) and Salerno airport. It is for people with a valid flight ticket that day. The fare is €12 each way. GESAC's [October 27th notice](https://www.aeroportodinapoli.it/documents/d/gesac/collegamenti-apt-salerno_27-10-26-pdf?download=true) says you can pay on board by card. The timetable shows about 1 hour 15 minutes, with eight trips a day each way. The first bus from Naples leaves at 5:00 a.m. and is due at the airport at 6:15 a.m.

GESAC also increased buses between Salerno airport and Salerno railway station, kept the existing links to Potenza, Eboli, Battipaglia, and the Amalfi Coast, and added an experimental shared taxi at a set fare between Salerno airport and the main stations in Naples and Salerno. It has not published that taxi fare on its traveler pages.

## What this means for travelers

If you are booked into or out of Naples in November, the runway at Capodichino is closed. The airline may have moved you to Salerno, replaced the flight with a bus, or changed or canceled the itinerary.

If you are connecting from Canada or the US, check the final Europe-to-Naples segment first. That ticket may now end at QSR, continue by bus from Rome Fiumicino, get canceled, or get rebooked another way.

If you are still booking a November trip to the Naples area, search the airline's current airport code, including QSR. GESAC's published window runs through November 30th. A Naples hotel still means a ground transfer from Salerno; GESAC says the Amalfi Coast buses from that airport are still running.

## What to do

1. Open the airline app or manage booking and check whether **your flight** was moved to QSR, replaced by ITA's Rome bus, or canceled.
2. If you are on a Salerno flight, build in the ground time. The special bus from Napoli Centrale takes about 1 hour 15 minutes, and the first arrival is 6:15 a.m. If the 6:15 a.m. arrival is too late for your flight, you need another transfer.
3. If ITA canceled or changed your ticket, use its rebooking and refund path first, including +39 06 85960020.

Where [EU passenger-rights rules](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm) apply, they cover reimbursement or rerouting if the airline cancels the flight. For a practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Capodichino is closed to flights through November 30th, 2026, for runway work. Some routes operate from Salerno. ITA's Rome link is a bus that still uses Capodichino. Check the airline before you go.
