On Sunday, September 13th, 2026, overlapping airport strikes hit Rome, Tuscany, Sardinia, and Venice. [ADR Security](https://www.adr.it/bsn-security) staff at Fiumicino and Ciampino are called out for 24 hours, so screening can slow for anyone going through those checkpoints.

Another Europe airport labor day after [Schiphol’s security stoppages](/news/schiphol-security-short-stoppages-ultimatum/) and [Barcelona’s Groundforce walkout](/news/barcelona-el-prat-groundforce-strike/). Italy’s [transport ministry strike register](https://scioperi.mit.gov.it/mit2/public/scioperi), updated Sunday, lists company-level walkouts at these airports, with different hours. Check your airline for your flight’s status.

## Who is walking out, and when

All times are local.

| Airport | Staff | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fiumicino and Ciampino | ADR Security | 00:00-24:00 |
| Fiumicino and Ciampino | Aviation Services | 11:00-15:00 |
| Pisa and Florence | Toscana Aeroporti, GH Toscana, Consulta | 11:00-15:00 |
| Cagliari Elmas | SOGAER, SOGAERSECURITY, SOGAERDYN | 13:00-17:00 |
| Alghero | SOGEAAL | 00:00-23:59 |
| Venice Marco Polo | Sicuritalia IVRI | 10:00-14:00 |

Milan’s airports are not on Sunday’s aviation list.

## Rome: Fiumicino and Ciampino

ADR Security runs passenger and baggage screening at Rome’s two airports. A 24-hour security walkout can stretch checkpoint lines for every airline using those terminals.

[Aviation Services](https://aviationservicesgroup.it/en/) is a ground-handling company at Fiumicino and Ciampino. Its staff walk out from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. If your airline uses that handler, that walkout may affect bag drop, boarding, and baggage claim.

## Tuscany, Sardinia, and Venice

At **Pisa** and **Florence**, airport and ground-handling staff at Toscana Aeroporti, GH Toscana, and Consulta are called out from 11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. USB’s Tuscany notice also includes a refusal of overtime from September 13th through October 12th, 2026.

At **Cagliari Elmas**, SOGAER, SOGAERSECURITY, and SOGAERDYN staff are on strike from 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. At **Alghero**, the SOGEAAL walkout is 24 hours.

At **Venice Marco Polo**, Sicuritalia IVRI security staff are on strike from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

## Which flights still operate

[ENAC](https://www.enac.gov.it/trasporto-aereo/diritto-alla-mobilita/scioperi-nel-trasporto-aereo/voli-garantiti/) still requires flights in the **7:00 a.m. to 10:00 a.m.** and **6:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.** local windows to operate during aviation strikes. Screening, check-in, and bags can still run slow in those hours.

ENAC’s published guaranteed-flight attachments on that page are still the July 21st, 2026 DAT and easyJet files. Use your airline’s status for today’s flight.

The ministry register currently lists USB’s easyJet flying-staff action for **October 15th**, not Sunday.

## What this means for travelers

If you fly out of Fiumicino or Ciampino today, the live risk is time through security, all day. If your airline also uses Aviation Services in the midday window, bag drop and boarding can slow in the same stretch.

If you fly Pisa, Florence, Cagliari, Alghero, or Venice today, match your departure to the hours above. A same-day tight connection through one of these airports has little slack if screening or handling slows.

If you are flying through Milan, or your ticket never uses one of the listed airports, this walkout set does not apply to you.

## What to do now

1. Check whether **your airport** is on the list, then match your local departure time to the hours above. For Rome security, that is the full day.
2. Check airline status before you leave for the airport. Finish online check-in when offered.
3. If your flight is delayed or canceled, use the airline’s rebooking and care path first.

## Bottom line

Sunday’s Italy airport strikes are several company walkouts at Rome, Tuscany, Sardinia, and Venice, with Rome security covering all day. Check your airport, your hours, and your airline status before you go.
