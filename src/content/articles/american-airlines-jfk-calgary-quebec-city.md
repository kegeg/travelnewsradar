American Airlines has started seasonal nonstops between **New York JFK** and **Calgary** and **Québec City**, restoring American’s JFK–Québec City service after a long gap. Québec City–JFK began **August 5th, 2026**. Calgary–JFK began **August 6th**. Both seasons are short, so the useful booking window is late summer and early fall only.

## The new flights

| Route | Start | Currently scheduled through | Frequency | Aircraft |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Québec City (YQB)–JFK | August 5th, 2026 | October 12th, 2026 | Daily | Embraer E175 (76 seats) |
| Calgary (YYC)–JFK | August 6th, 2026 | October 4th, 2026 | 3x weekly (Tue, Thu, Sun) | Boeing 737 MAX 8 (172 seats) |

Those dates and aircraft types match American’s Canada schedule language from its mid-July anniversary materials and current booking-tool schedule data. Always re-check [aa.com](https://www.aa.com/) for your exact travel day, because seasonal schedules can still move by a flight or two.

The Québec City flight is marketed as American and operated by Republic Airways with a regional Embraer E175. The Calgary flight is a mainline 737 MAX 8.

## How this fits American’s Canada network

For Calgary, the new JFK link sits beside American’s existing **Saturday-only LaGuardia–Calgary** flight, plus American service from Calgary into hubs such as Chicago, Charlotte, and Dallas-Fort Worth.

For Québec City, American already flies to hubs including Philadelphia, Chicago, Charlotte, and Dallas-Fort Worth. The new route is the JFK nonstop. American has said it is the only US airline with nonstop JFK–Québec City service.

Greater New York still has other Canada options on different airports and airlines. United Express, for example, has Newark–Québec City flying. On Calgary–JFK, WestJet already sells a nonstop. Air Canada serves Calgary–Newark. The traveler question is usually which New York airport and which connection you actually need.

## What this means for travelers

If you want a **nonstop between JFK and Calgary or Québec City** in August, September, or early October 2026, American now sells that itinerary. Nothing else about Canada entry rules or airport process changes because of this schedule add.

JFK connections matter more than the Canada city alone. American’s pitch is onward access across the United States and to Europe through JFK. That only helps if your connecting times and terminals work. Price the full trip, not just the new nonstop segment.

Preclearance also differs by city. [Calgary has US CBP preclearance](https://www.cbp.gov/travel/preclearance), so YYC–JFK passengers normally clear US entry before departure and can connect more like a domestic arrival. Québec City does not have US preclearance. YQB–JFK passengers clear US entry at JFK, so leave more time for immigration and Terminal 8 before any onward flight.

## Bottom line

American’s new JFK–Calgary and JFK–Québec City flights are real, bookable seasonal nonstops, currently scheduled through October 4th and October 12th, 2026. Use them if the airport pair fits your trip.
