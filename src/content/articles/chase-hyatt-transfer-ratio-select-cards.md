Chase Ultimate Rewards points now transfer to World of Hyatt at a 4:3 rate on select cards, including the Sapphire Preferred and Ink Business Preferred. [Chase's published example](https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred): 1,000 Ultimate Rewards points become 750 World of Hyatt points. On Ink Business Preferred, Ink Plus, and Corporate Flex, and on Sapphire Preferred cards applied before June 15th, the same Hyatt award takes more Chase points than it did through September 30th, 2026.

Hyatt points already in your World of Hyatt account are unchanged. A separate Hyatt story already on the site is the [Delta Dual Earn partnership](/news/delta-hyatt-dual-earn-loyalty-partnership/).

## Which cards use 4:3

Chase announced the Hyatt rate in its [June 15th, 2026 Sapphire Preferred update](https://media.chase.com/news/Meet-the-New-Chase-Sapphire-Preferred). The 4:3 rate is now on the [Sapphire Preferred benefits page](https://account.chase.com/sapphire/preferred/offers) and the [Ink Business Preferred product page](https://creditcards.chase.com/business-credit-cards/ink/business-preferred).

| Card | World of Hyatt transfer as of October 1st, 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| Sapphire Preferred, applied before June 15th, 2026 | 4:3. The 1:1 rate ran through September 30th |
| Sapphire Preferred, applied June 15th, 2026 or later | 4:3, in place since approval |
| Ink Business Preferred | 4:3 for existing accounts and for new applicants from October 1st |
| Ink Plus and Corporate Flex | 4:3 |
| Other hotel and airline partners on Sapphire Preferred | Still listed at 1:1 on the Preferred benefits page |

Chase's press release splits Sapphire Preferred by when you applied, and the live benefits page says opened your card account. If you applied around June 15th, check the live Hyatt rate in Ultimate Rewards before you transfer.

Chase processes partner transfers in 1,000-point increments. They are final. Chase says many post by the next business day and can take up to seven business days.

Chase listed those cards for the new Hyatt rate. It did not list the Sapphire Reserve or Sapphire Reserve for Business. Log in and read the live Hyatt rate in [Ultimate Rewards](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points) for the account you are sending from. Chase also says you may be able to move points between your own Chase cards, which matters if you hold a Reserve alongside a Preferred or Ink card.

Sapphire Preferred cardholders who applied before June 15th have a related same-day cutoff: eligible purchases through October 1st, 2026 still earn the old 10% anniversary bonus, awarded by January 31st, 2027.

## What this means for travelers

If you transfer from Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, Ink Plus, or Corporate Flex, four Chase points now become three Hyatt points. 80,000 Chase points become 60,000 Hyatt points.

If you earn on a Freedom card, move or combine those points into an eligible Sapphire or Ink account first. Ink Plus and Corporate Flex points may need that same step. The Hyatt rate is the one on the account you submit the transfer from.

If you do not stay at Hyatt, nothing in your next trip changes.

Sapphire Reserve and Sapphire Reserve for Business are widely reported to keep the 1:1 Hyatt rate after October 1st, though Chase has not published that rate on its own benefits pages. Confirm it in Ultimate Rewards before you transfer, and combine there first if it is better.

I hold a Sapphire Preferred from April 2025 and both Reserve cards. Caroline and I stay at Hyatts a lot.

Ultimate Rewards points do not expire as long as the Chase account stays open. Once you send them to World of Hyatt, they follow Hyatt's rules, and Chase will not reverse the transfer.

See [Flytrippers' current points valuations](https://flytrippers.com/how-much-are-points-worth-points-valuations-in-canada/) for Chase points and World of Hyatt points.

## What to do

1. Open Ultimate Rewards, pick the card you will transfer from, and read the live World of Hyatt rate before you send anything.
2. If you also have a Sapphire Reserve or Reserve for Business, confirm that account's live Hyatt rate and combine there first if it is better. That rate is widely reported to still be 1:1.
3. Transfer only what you need for a stay you can actually book. Transfers are final.

## Bottom line

Sapphire Preferred, Ink Business Preferred, Ink Plus, and Corporate Flex now transfer to World of Hyatt at 4:3, or 1,000 Chase points for 750 Hyatt points. Confirm the live rate on the account you transfer from, especially if you also hold a Reserve.
