WestJet is offering a one-time change or cancellation on eligible Puerto Vallarta flights October 18th and 19th as Hurricane Trudy moves toward west-central Mexico. The [advisory](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/get-travel-ready/advisories), posted October 15th, covers travel to or from Puerto Vallarta (PVR) for flight-only bookings made directly with WestJet. Puerto Vallarta's airport operator has not posted a Trudy closure.

This is the same check-your-booking habit as [Hurricane Isaias in Florida](/news/pensacola-destin-airports-closed-hurricane-isaias/) and [Hurricane Lala in Hawaii](/news/hurricane-lala-hawaii-flights-waivers/).

## What happened

The [National Hurricane Center](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/) said at 6 p.m. Central Saturday, October 17th, that Trudy's center was about 100 miles (160 km) southwest of Manzanillo and 175 miles (280 km) south of Cabo Corrientes, moving northwest near 10 mph (17 km/h). Maximum sustained winds were 100 mph (155 km/h). The forecast track takes the center near the southwestern and west-central Mexican coast Saturday night and Sunday.

A hurricane warning is in effect from Manzanillo north to Cabo Corrientes. Puerto Vallarta sits north of Cabo Corrientes, inside a tropical storm warning that covers the coast from there to San Blas and Las Islas Marias. Tropical storm conditions are expected in that mainland warning area later Saturday night. NHC also called for 4 to 8 inches of rain (about 10 to 20 cm) along the Michoacán, Colima, and Jalisco coast, with isolated totals around 12 inches (about 30 cm), and a dangerous storm surge with damaging waves in the warning areas.

[Grupo Aeroportuario del Pacífico](https://www.aeropuertosgap.com.mx/es/comunicados-generales) has not listed a Trudy closure for Puerto Vallarta. Its latest PVR notices are still the Hurricane Simon shutdown that began at 6 p.m. local time on October 10th and the staged reopen that put commercial flights back on October 13th. During Simon, GAP closed the airport after Mexico's civil aviation agency issued a NOTAM. Recheck GAP's notices if conditions worsen, and check your flight rather than assuming the airport is open or shut.

## What WestJet is offering

WestJet's advisory points to its [flexible change and cancellation policy](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/manage/change-cancel) for travel advisories. That policy applies to flights booked directly with WestJet, including UltraBasic.

| Item | Direct WestJet flight-only booking |
| --- | --- |
| Change fee | One-time $0 |
| Fare difference | Waived for a normal date or time shift; still applies if you change fare type, cabin, or destination |
| New trip timing | Travel must be completed within 60 days of the original departure |
| Cancel fee | One-time $0 for the entire booking |
| Refund vs credit | Follows your fare rules (cash refund or Travel Bank). UltraBasic gets a Travel Bank credit valid 12 months |
| Cutoff | More than 2 hours before departure |

Use [Manage Trips](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/manage) for a direct flight-only ticket. If the trip is a WestJet Vacations, Sunwing Vacations, or Vacances WestJet Québec package, contact that company. Agent, Expedia, AIR MILES, corporate, and other-airline tickets go back to whoever sold them.

Air Canada Vacations posted a separate [October 17th Puerto Vallarta hurricane notice](https://vacations.aircanada.com/en/plan-your-trip/travel-info/travel-advisories) for packages. Date changes there can add fees. If you choose not to travel, Air Canada Vacations says you can cancel for a future travel credit valid one year from the original departure.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold a WestJet ticket to or from Puerto Vallarta on October 18th or 19th, you can use the advisory to move or cancel once without the usual fee, as long as you booked direct and you stay inside the fare-difference and 60-day rules above. Use it if your plans are flexible and you want extra time, while that window is still open.

Open the WestJet app or Manage Trips before you leave for the airport. If WestJet cancels your flight, stay on its rebooking path first, including a partner flight it assigns. Wait for that offer before you buy a new ticket on another airline.

People already in Puerto Vallarta should watch flooding, mudslides, and dangerous surf, not just the drive to the airport. NHC's rain and surge notes cover Jalisco. If you are still booking after this weekend, nothing material should change.

If your ticket is on another airline, check that carrier's own alerts before you pay a change fee. WestJet's posted PVR dates apply only to WestJet tickets.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the WestJet app or Manage Trips.
2. If you booked direct, your dates are October 18th or 19th, and you want to move, change or cancel more than 2 hours before departure.
3. Call the vacation company or original seller if the booking is a package or a third-party ticket.
4. If WestJet canceled the flight, take its reaccommodation before you buy a new ticket on another carrier.
5. Keep receipts for meals or hotels if you get stuck overnight.

Weather disruptions on WestJet generally do not trigger a standalone cash payment under [Canada's passenger-rights rules](https://www.westjet.com/en-ca/interruptions). If the airline cancels and you choose not to travel, you can still rebook or request a refund of unused travel. If you paid with a card that includes flight-delay insurance, that coverage can still matter for hotel and meal costs after a long enough delay. For a practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

WestJet posted a Hurricane Trudy advisory for Puerto Vallarta travel on October 18th and 19th. Eligible direct flight-only bookings can make a one-time $0 change or cancellation in Manage Trips, with travel to finish within 60 days of the original departure. The airport operator has not posted a Trudy closure. Check your flight, and use the waiver before you pay a change fee.
