Argentina’s civil aviation authority is restricting commercial flying around Buenos Aires and Córdoba through **November 11th, 2026**, while Pope Leo XIV is in the country. Two ANAC notices are in force: two-hour takeoff and landing windows at **Aeroparque Jorge Newbery (AEP)**, **Córdoba Ingeniero Ambrosio Taravella (COR)**, and **Ezeiza Ministro Pistarini (EZE)**, and wider airspace zones that keep commercial flights grounded or rerouted for several hours at a time. Check the live booking even if your time sits outside an airport window.

Sunday’s Aeroparque airport window, 3:50 p.m. to 5:50 p.m. local, is already over. The next stretch is **Monday, November 9th**, when a Buenos Aires-area airspace zone runs 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. local and covers both Aeroparque and Ezeiza. Aeroparque also has a nested two-hour airport window, 11:15 a.m. to 1:15 p.m.

## When the restrictions apply

[ANAC AIP Supplement A 255/2026](https://ais.anac.gob.ar/descarga/sup-6ae896310bc45) lists the commercial airport windows in local Argentina time (UTC-3, two hours ahead of US Eastern):

| Airport | Date | Local window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aeroparque (AEP) | Sunday, November 8th | 3:50 p.m. to 5:50 p.m. |
| Aeroparque (AEP) | Monday, November 9th | 11:15 a.m. to 1:15 p.m. |
| Aeroparque (AEP) | Tuesday, November 10th | 6:30 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., and 5:10 p.m. to 7:10 p.m. |
| Córdoba (COR) | Tuesday, November 10th | 7:30 a.m. to 9:30 a.m., and 3:55 p.m. to 5:55 p.m. |
| Ezeiza (EZE) | Wednesday, November 11th | 1:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. |

Tuesday’s Aeroparque and Córdoba airport windows line up with the papal routing between those two airports. Wednesday’s Ezeiza window is the commercial airport restriction. General aviation at Ezeiza’s FBO is restricted all day Tuesday and until 3:00 p.m. Wednesday, which is a longer block than the passenger-terminal window.

A separate notice, [AIP Supplement A 249/2026](https://ais.anac.gob.ar/descarga/sup-6add4c367c1ea), dated October 24th, is still in force. It cancels an older order (A 248). A 255 adds the airport-level windows on top of A 249’s restricted-operation zones, where commercial and general aviation are not authorized to fly.

Those zones are 55-nautical-mile circles, about 102 km (63 miles), one centered on Ezeiza and one on Córdoba. Aeroparque sits inside the Buenos Aires zone with Ezeiza, so an Ezeiza flight on Monday, or an Aeroparque flight on Wednesday morning, can be hit even when that airport is missing from the two-hour table that day.

| Area | Date | Local window |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buenos Aires area (AEP and EZE) | Monday, November 9th | 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. |
| Córdoba area | Tuesday, November 10th | 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. |
| Buenos Aires area (AEP and EZE) | Tuesday, November 10th | 7:00 p.m. to 11:59 p.m. |
| Buenos Aires area (AEP and EZE) | Wednesday, November 11th | 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. |

On Wednesday, that Buenos Aires airspace window runs straight into Ezeiza’s 1:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. airport restriction, so Ezeiza is limited from 8:00 a.m. through 3:00 p.m. local. Aeroparque’s zone restriction ends at 1:00 p.m., and it has no separate airport window that day. Airports can still operate on either side of those hours.

Argentina’s [Defense Ministry](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/el-ministerio-de-defensa-reforzara-la-proteccion-aeroespacial-durante-la-visita-del-papa) says the airspace limits in the Buenos Aires and Córdoba terminal areas are partial and temporary, and that they run from November 8th through 11th. [Buenos Aires city](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/visita-apostolica-del-santo-padre-leon-xiv/cronograma-de-transporte-aereo) tells passengers that some flight times from Sunday through Tuesday may change, and to check the airline.

## What this means for travelers

Match the **airport code**, not just “Buenos Aires.” Aeroparque and Ezeiza also share the same Buenos Aires airspace zone on Monday, Tuesday evening, and Wednesday morning, so a time missing from one airport’s two-hour list can still be restricted. Aeroparque is the close-in city airport for most domestic hops and many regional South America flights. Ezeiza is the long-haul airport, about 30 km (19 miles) southwest of the center. A same-day AEP-EZE connection is a ground transfer even on a quiet day.

I used both airports a lot from Buenos Aires last year, and one of my Bariloche flights even moved from Aeroparque to Ezeiza. The ticket still said Buenos Aires, and the airport on it had changed.

A flight can sit outside an airport window and still fall inside an airspace restriction. Airlines can also shift flights, and delays can spill past a window. Córdoba’s airspace block is Tuesday morning into early afternoon, and it also has Tuesday airport windows. If you are on a US or Canada itinerary, the Argentina domestic or regional sector is the one to check first, including Copa, LATAM, Aerolíneas Argentinas, JetSMART, and other tickets that show AEP, EZE, or COR.

Separately, [American Airlines paused Ezeiza flying](https://saleslink.aa.com/en-US/blog/2026/reduction_suspension_of_service/ezeiza-runway-maintenance-5-15-2026.html) from **October 25th through November 11th** for runway work, with rebooking windows of October 18th to 24th or November 12th to 19th. That pause is the Ezeiza runway project. If your ticket was already on American to EZE this week, use that notice first.

Road access to Aeroparque can slow around papal events in Palermo. The city has posted approach routes.

## What to do

1. Open the booking, confirm the airport code (**AEP**, **EZE**, or **COR**) and the live airline status, and check both the airport windows and the airspace hours above.
2. If the airline already reissued the ticket, use that rebooking path first. Do not buy a same-day ticket on another carrier until the original airline says it will not move you.
3. If you are driving to Aeroparque, check the city’s posted access routes and build slack around the Monday and Tuesday event hours.
4. If a delay or cancellation leaves you overnight, keep receipts. Travel delay coverage on the card that paid for the ticket can help after a long enough delay. For the broader checklist, see [Flytrippers on canceled and delayed flights](https://flytrippers.com/most-important-things-related-to-canceled-and-delayed-flights/).

## Bottom line

ANAC’s airport windows and the wider Buenos Aires and Córdoba airspace restrictions run through November 11th, 2026. Check AEP versus EZE, then the live booking, before you leave for the airport.
