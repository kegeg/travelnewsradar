Air Canada will end its Vancouver (YVR) to Singapore (SIN) nonstop after **January 26th, 2027**. That is currently Canada's only nonstop to Singapore, and Air Canada's longest flight. Four other long-haul routes are also gone from later schedules, including Montreal-Algiers and Vancouver-Dubai.

## What Air Canada confirmed

Air Canada [launched Vancouver-Singapore](https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/air-canada-to-launch-new-vancouver-to-singapore-service-876477928.html) on **April 3rd, 2024**, four times a week on a Boeing 787. It later ran up to five times a week. The route covers **12,818 km (about 7,965 miles)**. Vancouver to Singapore takes about **16 hours 45 minutes**; the return is about **14 hours**.

The airline confirmed the service did not meet the business targets needed to keep it going after close to three years of investment. It pointed to demand, the ultra-long distance, and fuel burn on a flight that long. Air Canada said it picked an end date "far enough into the future to protect the majority of our customers," including the Christmas peak.

Flights remain on sale through late January. Schedule filings show the last Vancouver departure on **January 24th, 2027** (AC19) and the last Singapore departure on **January 26th, 2027** (AC20). Dates after that are already off sale.

Changi Airport Group said the suspension removes Changi's only direct air link to Canada. Air Canada says it will keep selling Singapore through Star Alliance codeshares, including Singapore Airlines flights via Tokyo Narita, Seoul Incheon, Hong Kong, London Heathrow, Frankfurt, and Brussels, plus ANA connections at Tokyo Narita and Haneda.

Summer 2027 filings still add **Vancouver-Guangzhou** three times a week from **May 4th, 2027**, on a 787-9.

## The other long-haul routes

The five routes were removed from later schedule filings at different times, rather than in a single Air Canada newsroom release.

| Route | What the schedule shows now |
| --- | --- |
| Vancouver-Singapore | Ends January 2027. Last YVR flight January 24th; last SIN flight January 26th. |
| Montreal-Algiers | Summer 2026 already skipped. A planned four-weekly A330 return from June 2nd, 2027, was pulled in August. |
| Toronto-Mumbai via Heathrow | Winter 2025/26 was the recent season. A filed London-Mumbai flight for October 25th, 2026, through March 26th, 2027, was later canceled. |
| Vancouver-Dubai | Last operated in March 2025. The following winter season was closed before it started. |
| Calgary-Delhi via Heathrow | Last ran as a winter 2024/25 daily Calgary-Heathrow-Delhi service. Later Calgary-Heathrow filings stop in London. |

Vancouver-Dubai is a separate route from Toronto-Dubai. Air Canada's [Middle East travel update](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/middle-east-travel.html), dated **September 24th, 2026**, keeps **Toronto-Dubai** canceled until the **end of March 2027**. Air Canada's [Toronto-Dubai booking page](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-from-toronto-to-dubai), checked **October 6th, 2026**, still advertises year-round nonstops and lists fares inside that window. Current schedule filings for the same period are still listed. Check the live booking before you rely on those dates. That's the same Air Canada Dubai pause as our earlier [Dubai and Tel Aviv suspension](/news/air-canada-dubai-tel-aviv-suspension/) notes, later updated when [Toronto-Tel Aviv moved up](/news/air-canada-toronto-tel-aviv-restart/). Vancouver-Dubai stays off the timetable.

Toronto-Delhi continues, and Montreal-Delhi is still a seasonal winter service in current filings.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold an Air Canada ticket on Vancouver-Singapore **after January 26th, 2027**, open the booking. Inventory after those dates is already off sale. Use Air Canada's rebooking path first rather than buying another ticket on your own. If the new routing does not work, Air Canada's [refund and cancellation policy](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/fly/customer-support/cancellation-refund.html) covers the unused portion when the airline canceled the flight.

If you are still planning a 2027 trip between Western Canada and Singapore, there will be no Air Canada nonstop after those January dates. One-stop options remain via Hong Kong, Taipei, Tokyo, Seoul, Bangkok, and other hubs. Codeshare itineraries can still show an Air Canada flight number on a partner-operated segment. Check the operating carrier before you buy.

Christmas 2026 and early January 2027 Vancouver-Singapore dates are still inside the operating window.

If you wanted Montreal-Algiers, Toronto-Mumbai, Vancouver-Dubai, or Calgary-Delhi on an Air Canada-operated flight in 2027, those routings are not in the current timetable. The Montreal-Algiers 2027 plan was pulled from filings in August; Air Canada could file it again. Price another airline or a connection that actually operates.

## What to do now

1. If your Air Canada ticket shows Vancouver-Singapore after January 26th, 2027, retrieve it in [Air Canada Trips](https://www.aircanada.com/home/ca/en/aco/trips) and take Air Canada's rebooking or refund path.
2. If you booked through an agent or another airline's ticket, contact the ticket issuer. Air Canada cannot always change a codeshare-issued booking.
3. If you are only shopping 2027 dates, search [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) for the connection you will actually fly, and book hotels around those connection times rather than a Vancouver-Singapore nonstop after late January.

## Bottom line

Air Canada will fly Vancouver-Singapore through **January 26th, 2027**, then drop Canada's only nonstop to Singapore. Ticketed travelers after that date should use Air Canada's rebooking or refund options. Montreal-Algiers and several other long-haul routes were already removed from later schedule filings, including Vancouver-Dubai, a separate route from Toronto-Dubai. Air Canada's September 24th travel update still has Toronto-Dubai canceled until the end of March 2027; confirm the live booking, because fares and filings for that window are still listed.
