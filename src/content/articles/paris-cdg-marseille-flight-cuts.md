France's civil aviation authority (DGAC) asked airlines to cut flights at Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Marseille-Provence on Tuesday, September 29th, 2026, during a nationwide public-sector strike. If you have booked a CDG flight during the day or a Marseille flight this evening, check your airline before you leave for the airport.

Another Europe airport labor disruption after [Italy's Sunday airport walkouts](/news/italy-airport-strikes-rome-tuscany-sardinia-venice/) and [Charleroi's night controller strike](/news/charleroi-airport-skeyes-night-atc-strike/). DGAC asked airlines Monday evening to reduce their programs so they could take flights out of Tuesday's schedule in advance.

## Which airports, and when

All times below are local. France is six hours ahead of Eastern Time on Tuesday.

| Airport | Published cut | Hours (local) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paris Charles de Gaulle | 5% of flights | 7:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m. |
| Marseille-Provence | 25% of flights | 4:00 p.m.-midnight |
| Nantes, Strasbourg, Limoges, Tarbes-Lourdes, Pau | Possible disruption | Some slots; no published share |
| Other French airports | Normal | Full day |

CDG's window is 1:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. Eastern. Marseille's evening window is 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern.

The DGAC said the reductions are meant to "limit disruption and guarantee the safety of air operations." Aviation unions relayed the public-sector strike call, which is over a civil-service pay freeze ahead of the budget.

[Paris Aéroport](https://www.parisaeroport.fr/en/passengers/flights/departures) posts CDG departures. [Marseille Provence Airport](https://www.marseille.aeroport.fr/vols-et-destinations/vols) posts Marseille flights. If yours is pulled, your airline is responsible for offering reimbursement or rerouting, which can include another carrier or another form of transport.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked into or out of CDG between 7:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m. local, most flights should still operate. Five percent is a small share of a hub that size, so confirm yours: the pulled flights will not operate as booked. CDG is the main transatlantic gateway for many US and Canadian trips to France, so a daytime long-haul can still fall in that window.

If you have booked into or out of Marseille-Provence from 4:00 p.m. local through midnight, the published cut is steeper: one in four flights in that evening block. Morning and early-afternoon Marseille trips are outside that window.

If you have booked into or out of Nantes, Strasbourg, Limoges, Tarbes-Lourdes, or Pau today, DGAC warned of possible disruption on some slots and did not publish a percentage. Check the airline.

If your ticket uses another French airport, including Paris Orly, DGAC said traffic is normal. Confirm your own flight if you connect through CDG or Marseille.

## What to do now

1. Check **your flight** in the airline app or manage booking before you leave for the airport. [Air France](https://www.airfrance.com/) is the largest operator at CDG.
2. If the airline cancels your flight, use its rebooking or refund path first, including any partner flight it assigns. Wait for that before you buy a seat on another carrier, or you may lose the refund path.
3. For flights covered by [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), you can choose reimbursement or re-routing, plus care (meals, and a hotel if you are stuck overnight). Cash compensation is a separate claim and is case-specific. An external strike may qualify as an extraordinary circumstance only if the airline proves the required link and that reasonable measures could not have avoided the disruption.

Keep receipts if you are stranded. Card flight-delay insurance on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the delay meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

DGAC asked airlines to cut 5% of CDG flights from 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. local and 25% at Marseille from 4:00 p.m. to midnight. Other French airports are listed as normal, with possible disruption at five regional fields. Check your airline before you go.
