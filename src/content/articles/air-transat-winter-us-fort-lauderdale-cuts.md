Air Transat’s planned winter return to Fort Lauderdale from **Montreal** and **Halifax** is gone from current airline schedule filings. For winter 2026/27, the only Air Transat–operated US route left in those filings is **Quebec City–Fort Lauderdale**, three times a week from mid-December. That comes from schedule databases. Air Transat has not issued a fresh press release about the change.

## What Transat announced vs what schedules show now

On **April 17th, 2026**, [Air Transat’s winter program announcement](https://transat.mediaroom.com/2026-04-17-Winter-2026-2027-A-Wave-of-New-Offerings-Sweeps-Across-Air-Transats-Network-to-the-South-and-Europe) said Florida was coming back after a summer pause, with Fort Lauderdale from Montreal, Quebec City, and Halifax.

| Route | April announcement | Current winter filings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Montreal (YUL)–Fort Lauderdale (FLL) | Up to **7** weekly from **November 16th, 2026** | **Canceled** |
| Halifax (YHZ)–Fort Lauderdale (FLL) | **1** weekly from **December 16th, 2026** | **Canceled** |
| Quebec City (YQB)–Fort Lauderdale (FLL) | **3** weekly from mid-November 2026 | Still planned, **3** weekly from **December 15th, 2026** |

Industry schedule trackers published the filing update on **July 21st, 2026**. Air Transat has not posted a matching media release explaining the change. The filings are the working timetable until the airline updates booking tools or publishes a correction.

## Why a thinner US schedule fits the Canada-US travel picture

Canadian leisure flying into the US has been soft since early 2025. [Statistics Canada’s June 2026 leading indicator](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260713/dq260713a-eng.htm) shows Canadian-resident return trips from the United States up **3.2%** from June 2025, but that gain sits on a weak base. Versus June 2024, trips were still down **28.7%**, including **25.0%** fewer return trips by air.

Air travel is the soft part of the latest month too: June 2026 return trips from the US by air fell **3.8%** year over year, while car trips rose **5.2%**. For a leisure airline deciding where to put winter A321s, Florida capacity from multiple Canadian gateways is an easy place to trim when demand stays below older peaks.

The numbers make a winter Florida trim look plausible in mid-2026, alongside the airline’s broader push toward sun markets outside the US.

## Can you still get to the US with Air Transat?

Yes, often through the [Porter Airlines codeshare](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/book/codeshare-flights). Air Transat sells Porter-operated flights under TS flight numbers, marked “Operated by” Porter when you book on airtransat.com. Porter’s [partner page](https://www.flyporter.com/en-ca/about-porter/our-airline-partners) describes the same link for Canada-US and connecting trips.

That matters for Montreal and Halifax travelers who expected a Transat nonstop to Fort Lauderdale. You may still see US cities on airtransat.com, but the flight can be operated by Porter with Porter product rules. Check the operating carrier before you buy, and read baggage and change rules for the airline that actually flies the segment.

If the booking is a VIPorter award on Porter, cash surcharges on awards are a separate issue from this schedule cut. See our [Porter $40 VIPorter fuel surcharge](/news/porter-viporter-fuel-surcharge/) note if you are redeeming points.

## What this means for travelers

If you were planning a winter nonstop on Air Transat from **Montreal or Halifax to Fort Lauderdale**, do not count on that operated TS flight. Build the trip around Quebec City–Fort Lauderdale, another Canadian carrier, a US connection, or a Porter-operated codeshare you are happy to fly.

If you only care about getting to South Florida, options still exist. If you specifically wanted Transat’s cabin, bags, and package ecosystem on a YUL or YHZ nonstop, that product is not in the current winter filings.

Cruise passengers using Fort Lauderdale as a port should recheck air plans early. A missing nonstop from Montreal or Halifax can force an extra connection day or a different Canadian gateway.

## What to do now

1. Search your exact dates on [airtransat.com](https://www.airtransat.com/) and confirm whether any Florida result is operated by Air Transat or Porter.
2. If Quebec City–Fort Lauderdale works for you, that is the operated TS US route still in the winter filings.
3. Compare Air Canada, Porter, WestJet, and US carriers for Montreal and Halifax. Price the full door-to-door time, not just the base fare.
4. If you already hold a booking that mentioned Montreal or Halifax Fort Lauderdale on Transat, open the reservation and contact Air Transat or your agent. Schedule filings do not automatically rebook you or trigger an airline email.
5. Watch for a Transat update. Filings can move again before winter sales fully settle.

## Bottom line

Air Transat’s April winter plan put Fort Lauderdale back on the map from Montreal, Quebec City, and Halifax. Current schedule filings keep only Quebec City–Fort Lauderdale as operated TS service to the US. Plan Montreal and Halifax Florida trips with other airlines or a Porter codeshare, and verify the operating carrier before you pay.
