skeyes, Belgium's air traffic control operator, says it cannot provide regular control at Belgian airports or in its airspace on **Monday, November 23rd, 2026**, from **7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.** local. [Brussels Airport](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passengers/contact/airlines) expects most arriving and departing flights to be canceled. If you have booked into or out of Brussels or Charleroi on Monday, check your airline before you leave for the airport.

Another Belgium airport labor story after [October's Alyzia cancellations at Brussels Airport](/news/brussels-airport-alyzia-cancellations/) and [Charleroi's night-tower walkout](/news/charleroi-airport-skeyes-night-atc-strike/). This one is a daytime air traffic control halt across the country.

## What's happening Monday

skeyes said Saturday that a group of controllers will join the national strike against government budget cuts. Staffing will not be enough to handle usual traffic safely, so it is suspending regular air traffic control between 7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. Belgian time. Only emergency and other safety-critical flights keep minimum services.

Belgium is six hours ahead of US Eastern, so that window is **1:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.** Eastern.

[Brussels Airport](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en/passengers/contact/airlines) posted the passenger notice after Saturday afternoon's skeyes word. Airlines will contact passengers. The airport board shows a cancellation only after the airline files it.

A Brussels Airport spokesman put Monday's planned passenger flying between 6:00 a.m. and 10:00 p.m. local at **429** flights and **59,500** passengers.

[Brussels Airlines](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/plan-your-trip/flight-information), the airport's largest airline, says Belgian airspace will be closed in that window, so most arriving and departing flights at Brussels Airport then will have to be canceled, including Brussels Airlines trips. The airline says it was told late Saturday afternoon, and that until a few hours earlier the expected impact at Brussels Airport was almost nothing. It will email affected passengers.

Brussels South Charleroi Airport says no regular takeoffs or landings will be possible from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. Ryanair is the main airline there. The airport tells passengers not to go there without confirmation from the airline.

The same 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. halt covers the Belgian airports skeyes controls.

## Getting to the airport

Brussels Airport says public transport can also be disrupted. Check [STIB/MIVB](https://www.stib-mivb.be/) and [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/) before you head to or from the airport.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked **into or out of Brussels Airport (BRU)** on Monday, most flights in the 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. local window are expected to cancel. That includes transatlantic trips that use BRU, and same-day Europe connections off those flights.

If you have booked **on Brussels Airlines**, use [current information](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/plan-your-trip/flight-information) and [My Bookings](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/my-bookings/retrieve-booking.html). The airline says it will contact you by email and rebook canceled trips.

If you have booked **at Charleroi (CRL)**, including Ryanair, plan on no regular flying in that 14-hour window. Charleroi's [destination list](https://charleroiairport.be/en/destinations) has no scheduled nonstop to the US or Canada. The airport sits about 30 miles (50 km) south of Brussels.

If your Monday flight is before 7:00 a.m. or after 9:00 p.m. local, it is outside the posted control halt. Confirm anyway. An aircraft that cannot get in or out during the day can still cancel an earlier or later trip.

Sunday and Tuesday are outside the posted window. A Monday cancel can still rebook you onto those days.

## What to do now

1. Check **your airline** before you leave for the airport. For Brussels Airlines, use [current information](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/plan-your-trip/flight-information) and [My Bookings](https://www.brusselsairlines.com/ca/en/my-bookings/retrieve-booking.html). For [Ryanair](https://www.ryanair.com/gb/en) at Charleroi, use the app. [Brussels Airport](https://www.brusselsairport.be/en) has a flight search on the homepage.
2. If the airline cancels your flight, use its rebooking or refund path first, including any alternative it emails you. Brussels Airlines says it will rebook canceled trips free of charge onto another flight to the same destination in the same class when it can. Wait for that before you buy a seat on another carrier, or the replacement fare may not be reimbursed.
3. For flights covered by [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), you can choose reimbursement or re-routing, plus care (meals, and a hotel if you are stuck overnight). Cash compensation is a separate claim. Airlines often argue that an air traffic control strike is outside their control.

Keep receipts if you are stranded. Trip-cancellation or interruption coverage on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the cancellation meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

skeyes is suspending regular Belgian air traffic control Monday from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. local. Brussels Airport expects most arrivals and departures canceled. Charleroi says nothing takes off or lands in that window. Check your airline before you go.
