Air Transat opened [Staria](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria) on [November 2nd, 2026](https://www.transat.com/en-CA/corporate/media/news-releases/124627), a free rewards program. Members earn 3, 5, or 7 points per dollar on eligible Air Transat or Porter flights, before taxes and airport fees, and can use those points for 10% to 100% of an eligible Air Transat fare.

If you do not fly Air Transat or Porter, nothing in your bookings changes, and a ticket you already hold keeps its fare. Join and add your member number before you fly if you want the points.

## How you earn points

[Air Transat’s earn page](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria/earn-points) publishes the table. Rates are per dollar spent before taxes and airport fees.

| Purchase | Points per dollar |
| --- | --- |
| Air Transat or Porter Eco Budget | 3 |
| Eco Standard or Club Standard | 5 |
| Eco Flex or Club Flex | 7 |
| Transat package (flight, hotel, and transfers) | 1 |
| Eligible extras (Option Plus, seat, checked bag, pre-ordered meal, Option Flex) | 5 |

You still earn on the cash slice of a points-plus-cash booking. Package flights do not also earn the flight table on top of the package rate.

No points on taxes, surcharges, car rentals, excursions, insurance, donations, or extras bought at the airport. Seat, bag, and Option Plus extras need to be added more than 48 hours before departure. Pre-ordered meals need more than 72 hours. Option Flex (packages only) can be added up to 48 hours after booking, for a departure more than 21 days out.

Flights have to be booked on Air Transat’s Canadian website and operated by Air Transat or Porter. You have to be a member at the time you fly, and the member number has to be on the booking before that flight. Unused segments earn nothing. Points usually show a few hours after you fly. On a round trip, some land after the outbound and the rest after the return; on a package, all of it lands after the return. [Air Transat’s missing-points form](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/forms/staria-points-claim) asks you to wait 7 days before you file, or 30 days if you were moved onto another airline from an eligible Transat booking.

## How you use points

On the live [redeem page](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria/redeem-points) and [FAQ](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria/faq), points buy a share of the current fare. There is no published cents-per-point value and no date blackout. Sign in at booking.

| Booking | Points can cover | Still cash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flights operated entirely by Air Transat | 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, or 100% of the fare and carrier surcharge | Taxes, airport fees, and other charges |
| Transat packages | At least 10%, up to 40% of the package total | The rest, plus taxes and fees |

Redeem on the Canadian versions of airtransat.com (flights) and transat.com (packages). The booking pages currently require every flight on the itinerary to be Air Transat-operated, so a Porter-operated hop can earn and still be ineligible to redeem. Air Transat’s [terms](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria/terms-and-conditions) also mention some partner itineraries.

Points apply to the whole reservation, not one passenger. They do not combine with a promo code or a travel credit. Package bookings paid partly in points need full payment at checkout, with no deposit option.

Change and cancel rules follow the cash fare. For now, Air Transat says to call the contact center to change a points booking.

## Stars and constellations

Stars are a separate track from points. Members 18 and over complete missions in the member space (profile, survey, newsletter, a trip, a referral) and then pick a reward such as seat selection, a Bistro meal, or bonus points. You have 30 days to choose, or Air Transat assigns a default. Stars do not expire. Junior members cannot earn them.

A constellation is a shared points balance for up to nine members, run by an adult manager. The manager sets each person to earn only, or earn and redeem. When you join, your points move into the shared pool. Redemptions come out in proportion to each person’s share. You can leave and take what is left of your contribution, then you wait 6 months before joining or creating another group. Children 2 and over join as junior members through a parent.

Points expire after 36 consecutive months with no earn or redeem activity. After 7 years of inactivity, Air Transat can close the account.

## What this means for travelers

If you already buy Air Transat sun or Europe trips a couple of times a year, join before the next one and put the number on the booking. Occasional flyers use the same earn table as people who fly every month.

If you fly Porter, the same 3/5/7 table applies when the flight is Porter-operated and you add Staria before you fly. You redeem those points on Air Transat-operated trips, not on Porter, in the current redeem flow. If you already collect [VIPorter points](https://flytrippers.com/rewards-programs-overview/), that is a separate Porter currency that can also book some Air Transat flights.

Desjardins and Visa are named as future Staria card partners, with details due later in 2027. There is no fee, earn rate, or welcome bonus to run yet. If you have a Transat trip this winter, join now rather than waiting on those cards.

Enrollment is free for anyone 2 and over, but the online form currently lives on the Canadian sites and asks for a Canadian address. The [FAQ](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria/faq) says people without a Canadian address can still join through Air Transat’s rewards assistance form.

## What to do

1. If you fly Air Transat or Porter and want the points, [join Staria](https://www.airtransat.com/en-CA/staria) on the Canadian site (or use the assistance form if you have no Canadian address).
2. Add the member number in Manage My Booking before you fly, including tickets you already hold.
3. If you never fly Air Transat or Porter, you can skip this.

## Bottom line

Staria is live and free. Earn 3 to 7 points per dollar on eligible Air Transat or Porter flights, and spend them as 10% to 100% of an eligible Transat fare. Add your number before you fly.
