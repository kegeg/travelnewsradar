As of October 15th, 2026, Air Canada charges a non-refundable CA$30 (about US$21) plus tax per passenger on new bookings completed by phone with a Contact Centre agent. That includes Aeroplan flight rewards. [Book on aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) or in the Air Canada app and there is no booking fee.

If you make the new booking online, you do not pay this fee.

## Who pays

Air Canada's [Contact Centre booking fee page](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/contact-centre-booking-fee.html) and its [optional services list](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/legal/products-and-services.html) both apply the fee to a new reservation completed by phone with an Air Canada agent. Two passengers on the same new phone booking pay CA$60 plus tax (about US$42).

The fee shows as its own line on the receipt. It is not built into the airfare or the carrier-imposed charges. If you later cancel the ticket, Air Canada will not refund this booking fee.

| Situation | Booking fee |
| --- | --- |
| New booking on aircanada.com or the Air Canada app | None |
| New paid booking by phone with an agent | CA$30 plus tax per passenger, non-refundable |
| New Aeroplan flight reward by phone | Same CA$30 plus tax per passenger |
| Help with an existing reservation | This booking fee does not apply (fare change or cancel fees still can) |
| Previously booked trip delayed, canceled, or otherwise disrupted | This booking fee does not apply |
| Accessibility or other specialized care | Exempt |
| Travel agency booking | The agency sets its own fees |

If the website or app will not finish a booking and you call 1-888-247-2262 to complete it, Air Canada says the fee may still apply, depending on the circumstances.

## Aeroplan reward bookings

The same phone fee already applied to Aeroplan flight rewards booked through the Contact Centre. Paid tickets now use that same Contact Centre fee. [Aeroplan's flight reward policy](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/legal/aeroplan-flight-reward-policy.html) still lists a non-refundable CA$30 Contact Centre fee, with online Aeroplan bookings free. If a reward cannot be completed online, that policy still charges the Contact Centre fee.

Partner awards are a separate charge. Rewards that include a flight operated by an airline other than Air Canada, Air Canada Express, Air Canada Rouge, or a Landline bus segment pick up a CA$39 partner booking fee per ticket, including when you book online.

Aeroplan reward change and cancellation fees are also separate, and some of those fees are higher if you manage the ticket by phone instead of on aircanada.com.

## What this means for travelers

The fee stacks per passenger. A couple on one new phone booking pays CA$60 plus tax, on top of the fares.

Calling about a reservation you already have does not add this booking fee. Change or cancellation fees in your fare still apply. If that trip is delayed or canceled, Air Canada says Contact Centre help for the disruption does not include this fee, and many eligible changes can also be done in the app.

Accessibility support is exempt. Call Accessibility Services at 1-800-667-4732, or use Air Canada's [accessibility services](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/plan/accessibility.html) page.

If you book through a travel agent, ask that agent what they charge. Air Canada does not publish a matching phone fee for those bookings.

## What to do

1. Make new Air Canada and Aeroplan bookings on [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) or in the app when you can.
2. If you need accessibility support, call Accessibility Services at 1-800-667-4732. That path is exempt from the booking fee.
3. If an existing flight is delayed or canceled, use the app or site first. Calling about that disruption does not add this booking fee.

## Bottom line

A new Air Canada booking completed by phone now costs CA$30 plus tax per passenger, with no refund if you cancel. Website and app bookings stay free of that fee.
