American Airlines [published its summer 2027 international route package](https://news.aa.com/news/news-details/2026/Summer-gets-sweeter-with-new-global-routes-from-American-Airlines-NET-RTS-08/default.aspx) with **seven new international routes** and **three new destinations**. The main leisure adds are first-ever American service to **Vienna** and **Porto**, a return to **Reykjavik**, and new **JFK** nonstops to **Amsterdam** and **Nice**. American says all of the new summer 2027 routes will be **daily**. Tickets go on sale **August 31st, 2026** on [aa.com](https://www.aa.com/) and the American app.

Another American schedule story after [seasonal JFK flights to Calgary and Québec City](/news/american-airlines-jfk-calgary-quebec-city/). This one is mostly Europe for 2027.

## The seven new routes

| Route | Starts | Aircraft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chicago (ORD)–Tokyo Narita (NRT) | March 19th, 2027 | Boeing 787-9 |
| Philadelphia (PHL)–Porto (OPO) | March 28th, 2027 | Airbus A321XLR |
| New York (JFK)–Amsterdam (AMS) | March 28th, 2027 | Airbus A321XLR |
| Philadelphia (PHL)–Vienna (VIE) | May 6th, 2027 | Airbus A321XLR |
| New York (JFK)–Nice (NCE) | May 6th, 2027 | Airbus A321XLR |
| Charlotte (CLT)–Barcelona (BCN) | May 27th, 2027 | Boeing 777-200ER |
| Philadelphia (PHL)–Reykjavik (KEF) | May 27th, 2027 | Airbus A321neo |

American also plans a **fourth daily** JFK–London Heathrow flight from **March 28th, 2027**, on a Boeing 787-9 with Flagship Suite seats. That is an extra frequency on an existing city pair, not one of the seven new routes.

The same release says returning Charlotte–Paris and Miami–Milan seasons will both launch on **March 4th, 2027**. Recheck aa.com for your exact travel day; seasonal schedules this far out can still move.

## New destinations and Europe highlights

**Porto** and **Vienna** are new American destinations, and **Reykjavik** returns for the first time since 2019, all three from Philadelphia.

American says it will be the **only US airline** flying to Vienna when the route starts. The PHL–Vienna season is extended through **early January 2028**, so Christmas-market travel is inside the published window.

JFK–Amsterdam sits beside American’s existing Amsterdam flying from Philadelphia (year-round) and Dallas-Fort Worth (seasonal). JFK–Nice adds a second US gateway to the French Riviera beside American’s [existing Philadelphia–Nice service](https://www.nice.aeroport.fr/en/news/fourth-direct-flight-from-nice-to-new-york-with-american-airlines). Charlotte–Barcelona gives American a sixth US gateway into Barcelona, per the airline’s release.

Four of the Europe adds use American’s long-range Airbus **A321XLR**: Philadelphia–Porto, Philadelphia–Vienna, JFK–Amsterdam, and JFK–Nice.

## What this means for travelers

If you want a **US nonstop to Vienna, Porto, Reykjavik, Nice, or a new JFK–Amsterdam link** in summer 2027, American now has published start dates and aircraft types. Nothing about Schengen entry rules, EES queues, or passport requirements changes because of this schedule add. You still need the right documents for your nationality and enough connection time if Philadelphia, JFK, Charlotte, or Chicago is only a hub.

Sale day is **August 31st, 2026**. That is when inventory is supposed to open. Cheap seats on every date are not guaranteed. Price the full trip, including connections, not just the new nonstop segment.

Vienna is the one route where American already published an extended end window (through early January 2028). For routes without a published end date, use the booking tool’s displayed last dates before you lock hotels or onward tickets around a start or end day.

## Bottom line

American’s summer 2027 package adds seven daily international routes, including first service to Vienna and Porto, a Reykjavik return, and new JFK flights to Amsterdam and Nice. Tickets are set to go on sale August 31st, 2026 on [aa.com](https://www.aa.com/). Search your dates there before you build a trip around a seasonal start.
