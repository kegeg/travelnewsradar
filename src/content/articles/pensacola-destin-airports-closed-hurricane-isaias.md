Hurricane Isaias made landfall near Destin, Florida, around 8:30 p.m. Central on Friday, October 9th, with 105 mph (165 km/h) winds. Pensacola International Airport (PNS) is still FAA-closed Saturday, Destin-Fort Walton Beach Airport (VPS) remains shut until further notice, and Delta, American, and Southwest have fee-free change tools for eligible Gulf tickets.

This is the same check-your-booking habit as [Hurricane Lala's Hawaii airport closures](/news/hurricane-lala-hawaii-flights-waivers/) and [Northeast storm weekend waivers](/news/northeast-storm-weekend-flight-waivers/).

## What happened

The [National Hurricane Center](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/al09/al092026.update.10100129.shtml) said Isaias came ashore near Destin as a Category 2 hurricane. A NOAA gauge in Panama City Beach reported water about 5.6 feet (1.7 meters) above mean higher high water around landfall.

By 4 a.m. Central Saturday, the center had moved inland near Montgomery, Alabama, with maximum sustained winds down to 35 mph (55 km/h). The NHC's [last public advisory](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/text/MIATCPAT4.shtml) discontinued storm-surge and tropical-storm warnings. Rain of 4 to 8 inches (about 10 to 20 cm) is still possible across the Florida Panhandle, with localized amounts up to 15 inches (about 38 cm).

| Airport | Saturday morning status |
| --- | --- |
| Pensacola (PNS) | FAA closed to scheduled flights, listed through 6 p.m. Eastern |
| Destin-Fort Walton Beach (VPS) | Airport: closed until further notice (since 9 p.m. Thursday) |
| Panama City (ECP) | Commercial flights stopped 1 p.m. Central Friday; FAA page showed on time Saturday morning |

As of about 9:27 a.m. Eastern Saturday, the [FAA National Airspace System board](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) and [Pensacola airport-status page](https://www.faa.gov/airport-status/PNS) still showed PNS closed to scheduled operations. That 6 p.m. Eastern end time can move, and it is not a promise that airline flights restart then.

[Destin-Fort Walton Beach's website](https://www.flyvps.com/) still says all airport operations closed at 9 p.m. local time Thursday, October 8th, until further notice. The FAA's VPS status page did not list a matching closure NOTAM this morning. Use the airport's own notice and your airline app together.

Northwest Florida Beaches International Airport in Panama City [stopped commercial flights at 1 p.m. Central Friday](https://www.iflybeaches.com/documents/hurricane-isaias-official-statement-october-9-2026). That statement said the terminal would stay open but was not operating as a public shelter. The FAA's ECP page showed on time Saturday morning and did not list a closure NOTAM. Confirm your flight rather than assuming the airport is open or still shut.

## What this means for travelers

If you are booked to, from, or through Pensacola or Destin-Fort Walton Beach this weekend, do not leave for the airport until the airline shows the flight as operating. Pensacola is still on an FAA closure. Destin has not posted a reopen time.

If the airline canceled your flight, stay on its rebooking path first (app, manage booking, airport desk once it reopens, or a partner flight it assigns). Wait for that offer before you buy a new ticket on another carrier. If your flight is still showing, use a posted waiver only if you want to move dates while the fee-free window is still open.

People already on the western Panhandle should watch flooding, downed trees, and road closures, not just the drive to the airport. Water levels along the Florida Gulf Coast were expected to recede Saturday, but flash flooding can continue. Check local emergency management and [FL511](https://fl511.com/) for roads.

If you are still booking a trip after this weekend, check the live airline schedule and whether the airport has reopened before you count on a posted departure.

Waiver city lists also cover other Gulf airports such as Mobile, Gulfport/Biloxi, Birmingham, and New Orleans. Those airports were not on Saturday morning's FAA closure list the way Pensacola was. Check your specific ticket.

## Airline waivers still in play

Rules differ by ticket purchase date, original travel date, allowed new dates, cabin, and city pair. Open manage booking before you pay a change fee.

| Airline | Original travel | Change by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| American | October 9th-11th | October 11th |
| Delta | October 9th-10th | October 13th |
| Southwest | VPS, ECP, PNS October 8th-11th | within 14 days of original date |

[Delta's Hurricane Isaias waiver](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/weather-alerts/tropical-storm-isaias) covers travel to, from, or through Pensacola, Panama City, Destin-Fort Walton Beach, Montgomery, Mobile, Gulfport/Biloxi, Dothan, Columbus (Mississippi), and Birmingham on October 9th and 10th. Tickets must have been issued on or before October 7th. You can change the flight on or before October 13th, and the new departure must also be on or before October 13th in the same cabin for the fare difference to be waived, or cancel for an eCredit. Changing origin or destination can cost extra. New Orleans is not on Delta's list.

[American's Isaias alert](https://www.aa.com/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) (current as of October 9th) covers American-operated flights in any fare class, including Basic Economy, to, from, or through Birmingham, Destin/Fort Walton Beach, Gulfport/Biloxi, Huntsville, Mobile, Montgomery, New Orleans, Panama City, Pensacola, and Tampa. Tickets must have been bought by October 6th. Scheduled travel is October 9th through 11th. New travel must be October 7th through 14th between the same cities, in the same cabin or with a fare difference. Changes must be booked by October 11th.

[Southwest's Hurricane Isaias advisory](https://www.southwest.com/travel-advisory/) covers Destin/Fort Walton Beach, New Orleans, Panama City Beach, and Pensacola on October 8th through 11th, Birmingham on October 9th through 11th, Atlanta on October 10th and 11th, and Nashville on October 10th. Eligible customers may rebook or travel standby within 14 days of the original travel date between the original city pairs without paying a change in airfare, or keep the existing reservation. If Southwest cancels or significantly delays the flight and you choose not to travel, refund rules on that page still apply.

If you hold a United ticket, confirm any window on your own reservation or [United's travel alerts page](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) before you pay a change fee.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the airline app or manage booking before you go to the airport.
2. Recheck [FAA NAS status](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) for Pensacola and [flyvps.com](https://www.flyvps.com/) if Destin is on your itinerary.
3. Look for a travel waiver in manage booking before you pay a change fee. American's listed change deadline is October 11th.
4. If the airline canceled your flight, take its reaccommodation (including partners) before you buy a new ticket on another carrier.
5. Keep receipts for meals or hotels in case your airline or insurance provider asks for them.

US weather cancellations and significant delays are a different conversation from a simple delay under [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds). If you paid with a card that includes flight-delay insurance, that coverage can still matter for hotel and meal costs after a long enough delay. For the practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

Hurricane Isaias came ashore near Destin on Friday night. Pensacola is still FAA-closed Saturday, and Destin-Fort Walton Beach remains shut until further notice. Panama City stopped commercial flights Friday afternoon; confirm that ticket rather than guessing from the FAA board. Delta, American, and Southwest have fee-free change tools for eligible Gulf travel, with different cities and dates. Check your flight, use the airline's rebooking path first, and use a waiver in manage booking before you pay change fees. American's listed change deadline is October 11th.
