Eligible Chase Ultimate Rewards cards can now transfer points 1:1 to Cathay Pacific Asia Miles and Qantas Frequent Flyer. Chase's public partner lists, including the [Sapphire Preferred benefits page](https://account.chase.com/sapphire/preferred/offers) and the [how-to transfer page](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points), still omit both programs. Open Ultimate Rewards and confirm them on the account you will transfer from before you send anything.

Chase already [cut World of Hyatt transfers to 4:3](/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-select-cards/) on select cards. These airline transfers are 1:1 on eligible cards.

## The 1:1 transfers

| Item | As of November 18th, 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| From | Chase Ultimate Rewards on a transfer-eligible card |
| To | Cathay Pacific Asia Miles, and Qantas Frequent Flyer |
| Rate | 1:1. 1,000 Chase points become 1,000 Asia Miles or 1,000 Qantas Points |
| Increments | 1,000 points |
| Reversible | No |
| Public Chase partner lists | Still omit Cathay and Qantas |
| Confirm | Ultimate Rewards after you sign in |

Chase's how-to page names Sapphire Preferred, Sapphire Reserve, and Ink Business Preferred as the cards that can transfer to travel partners. Sapphire Reserve for Business is widely treated as a transfer card in Ultimate Rewards. Confirm the partners on the account you will send from. If you earn on a Freedom card, move those points into one of those accounts first.

Chase processes partner transfers in 1,000-point increments. They are final. Chase says many post by the next business day and can take up to seven business days. The name on your Chase account has to match the name on the Asia Miles or Qantas account.

You need a [Cathay membership](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/membership/sign-up.html) and a [Qantas Frequent Flyer](https://www.qantas.com/en-us/frequent-flyer/join) account before you transfer. Qantas says a joining fee may apply.

Chase has not published a transfer bonus for these two programs.

## What this means for travelers

If you do not have a US Chase card that can transfer Ultimate Rewards, nothing in your next trip changes.

If you already book Oneworld awards with British Airways or Iberia Avios, you now have two more programs to compare on the same flight, including the cash portion.

Cathay's own award space is often easier to find in Asia Miles than through a partner. Qantas Frequent Flyer is the path for Qantas flights and some partner awards. Search the seat, then transfer only what you need.

I hold a Sapphire Preferred and both Reserve cards. I flew Cathay from Bangkok to Hong Kong in 2024, and I've flown Qantas in Australia and across the Pacific. I would only move Chase points after I had a seat I could actually book.

Ultimate Rewards points do not expire as long as the Chase account stays open. Once you send them, they follow Cathay's or Qantas's rules, and Chase will not reverse the transfer.

See [Flytrippers' current points valuations](https://flytrippers.com/how-much-are-points-worth-points-valuations-in-canada/) for Chase points, Asia Miles, and Qantas Points.

## What to do

1. Sign in to [Ultimate Rewards](https://www.chase.com/personal/credit-cards/education/basics/how-to-transfer-chase-ultimate-rewards-points), open Transfer to Travel Partners, and confirm Cathay and Qantas at 1:1 on the account you will send from.
2. Join [Asia Miles](https://www.cathaypacific.com/cx/en_US/membership/sign-up.html) and/or [Qantas Frequent Flyer](https://www.qantas.com/en-us/frequent-flyer/join) if you do not already have accounts, and match the name to your Chase account.
3. Find a bookable award, then transfer only the points you need.

## Bottom line

Eligible Chase Ultimate Rewards cards can now transfer 1:1 to Cathay Asia Miles and Qantas Frequent Flyer. Confirm both in Ultimate Rewards before you transfer, and find the award first.
