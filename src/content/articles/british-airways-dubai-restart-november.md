British Airways has moved its London Heathrow (LHR) to Dubai (DXB) restart to **November 3rd, 2026**, after the route had been listed for October 25th. If you hold a British Airways ticket that still shows an October Dubai flight, open the booking.

Another Dubai schedule story after [United’s delayed Newark-Dubai restart](/news/united-newark-dubai-restart-delayed-march-2027/). Air Canada still keeps Dubai canceled until [mid-January 2027](/news/air-canada-toronto-tel-aviv-restart/).

## What British Airways changed

British Airways plans one daily London-Dubai flight this winter from November 3rd, then two daily flights in summer. That is thinner than the three daily flights it usually ran on the route.

Abu Dhabi stays suspended until the winter 2027-2028 season.

The airline said it made further changes to its flying schedule “to provide greater clarity” for customers, due to the ongoing situation in the Middle East. It says it is keeping the situation under review and is in direct contact with affected customers to offer a range of options. British Airways suspended Middle East flying on **February 28th, 2026**.

The same mid-September revision currently lists these other dates. Recheck [ba.com](https://www.britishairways.com/) before you lock hotels around a single day. Schedules can still move.

| Route | Current British Airways plan |
| --- | --- |
| Dubai | November 3rd, 2026; one daily this winter, two daily in summer |
| Abu Dhabi | Suspended until winter 2027-2028 |
| Doha | Back on September 1st; one daily, down from two |
| Tel Aviv | October 25th; one daily |
| Riyadh | November 3rd; one daily |
| Amman | November 4th; four flights a week |
| Bahrain | Suspended until the end of March 2027 |

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked on British Airways to or from Dubai before November 3rd, that flight is still inside the canceled window. Use British Airways’ available rebooking or refund options first, before you buy a second ticket on your own.

If you were planning around October 25th, that restart is gone. November 3rd is now the first date British Airways is pointing to for its own Dubai service, and that date can still slip.

Abu Dhabi on British Airways stays off until winter 2027-2028. If you are booking new travel there this winter, look for another airline. If you already hold a British Airways Abu Dhabi ticket, contact British Airways or use Manage My Booking for the options that apply.

Check the operating carrier and your flight status. Dubai International Airport itself is still open, with limited and changing service from other carriers.

[Air Canada’s Middle East travel update](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/middle-east-travel.html), dated September 15th, 2026, still keeps Dubai canceled until mid-January 2027. That is a different airline and a different ticket.

[EASA’s conflict-zone bulletin for Persian Gulf airspace](https://www.easa.europa.eu/en/domains/air-operations/czibs/czib-2026-07r2) (CZIB-2026-07R2) stays valid through **September 30th, 2026**. It advises EASA operators not to fly over Persian Gulf waters except as needed for arrivals and departures. That helps explain why several European carriers still keep UAE flying thinned or delayed even when DXB is open.

[Global Affairs Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/united-arab-emirates) currently tells Canadians to avoid non-essential travel to the United Arab Emirates, last updated **September 15th, 2026**. The US advisory is **Level 3: Reconsider Travel** on the [State Department UAE page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/united-arab-emirates-travel-advisory.html). A [US Embassy UAE update](https://ae.usembassy.gov/travel-advisory-united-arab-emirates-level-3-reconsider-travel-august-29-2026/) on **August 29th, 2026** reported no change to the Level 3 rating. Advisories are one planning input. Recheck them before you keep a leisure trip on the books.

## What to do now

1. Open your reservation on [ba.com](https://www.britishairways.com/) or [Manage My Booking](https://www.britishairways.com/travel/managebooking/public/en_us) and confirm whether a British Airways Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or other listed Middle East flight is still on the ticket, and on which date.
2. If British Airways has canceled or moved your flight, take its rebooking or refund path first. The airline says it is offering affected customers a range of options. Wait for that before you buy a second ticket.
3. Recheck Canada’s [UAE advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/united-arab-emirates) and the [US Level 3 advisory](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/united-arab-emirates-travel-advisory.html) the same day you decide to keep or drop a leisure trip.

## Bottom line

British Airways now plans to restart London-Dubai on November 3rd, 2026, once a day this winter, with a second daily flight in summer. Abu Dhabi stays off until winter 2027-2028. If you hold a ticket, manage it on ba.com before you buy another one.
