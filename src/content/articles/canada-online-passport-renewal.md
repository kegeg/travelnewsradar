On **July 28th, 2026**, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada [made online passport renewal available](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/news/2026/07/canada-expands-online-passport-renewal-to-all-eligible-adults.html) to **all eligible adult Canadians in Canada** and removed the daily limit on online applications. If your passport is expired or within six months of expiry, you may be able to renew without mailing a paper package or visiting Service Canada.

Online renewals already existed in a limited form. What changed is the **daily application cap**. Eligible applicants can start when they are ready, instead of waiting for a free slot.

## Who can renew online

Online rules are [different from mail or in-person renewal](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/renew-adult-passport/submit-form-fees/renew-online.html). You need all of the following:

- You are renewing **your own** regular (blue) adult passport.
- Your **home and mailing addresses are in Canada**, and you will be there to receive the new passport.
- Your current passport is **expired**, or it will expire in the **next 6 months**.
- You do **not** need a passport for the next **20 business days**, plus mailing time.
- You applied for your current passport when you were **16 or older**.
- That passport was issued in the **last 15 years**, was valid for **5 or 10 years**, and shows your place of birth.
- You will keep the **same name, date of birth, place of birth, and gender identifier**.
- Your current passport is not damaged, seized, or surrendered, and it has **no observations** (extra notes some passports carry for special circumstances).
- You are not trying to renew after finding a previously lost or stolen passport that was never returned to the government.

You also need a **digital photo** taken in person by a commercial photographer within the last six months. A scan of a printed photo does not count.

## Rules to be aware of

Your current passport is **canceled when you apply**. After you submit the online renewal, the passport you hold is no longer valid. IRCC says if you have travel plans, apply in person. Online is only for people who can go without a passport for about **20 business days** plus mailing.

Online renewal does not cover applicants under 16, and it does not deliver a renewed passport outside Canada. Canadians abroad still apply by mail or at a Government of Canada office abroad.

If your passport expires in more than six months, you cannot renew online yet.

Online renewals use the same **20-business-day** processing standard as most simplified renewals in Canada, plus mailing. For 10-business-day, express, or urgent pickup, go in person to a Service Canada Centre or passport office that offers those services.

## Fees and the 30-day guarantee

In-Canada adult renewal fees [as of March 31st, 2026](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/fees/fee-changes-passport.html):

| Passport | Fee (CAD) |
| --- | --- |
| 5-year adult | **$122.50** |
| 10-year adult | **$163.50** |

Online renewals are paid digitally through the [IRCC Portal – New Version](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/renew-adult-passport/submit-form-fees/renew-online.html). Mail and in-person applications still cannot be paid that way.

Separately, for complete applications finished on or after **April 1st, 2026**, if processing takes more than **30 business days**, IRCC says you [get a full fee refund automatically](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/fees/passport-travel-document-refunds.html). That clock starts when the complete application is received. It does not include mailing time, extra review on complex files, or time spent waiting while IRCC asks for more information. Plan on the normal **20 business days** plus mail anyway.

## What this means for travelers

If you are a Canadian in Canada, your passport is expired or within six months of expiry, and you have no trips inside the processing window, online renewal is now an option: application, photo file, and payment in one portal, with status updates while it processes.

If you are a Canadian living outside Canada without a Canadian mailing address for delivery, online renewal still does not apply. Same for anyone who needs the passport sooner than the standard window.

## What to do

1. Check the official [who can renew online](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports/renew-adult-passport/submit-form-fees/renew-online.html) list before you create an account.
2. Look at your next travel dates. If you need the passport inside the next few weeks, use in-person service.
3. Get a commercial digital passport photo (and keep a printed set as backup if you might switch to mail or in person).
4. Confirm your addresses are in Canada and that you can receive mail there.
5. Start the renewal in the **IRCC Portal – New Version** only when the checklist above is clean.

## Bottom line

As of July 28th, 2026, eligible adults in Canada can renew a passport online without a daily application cap. Processing is still about **20 business days** plus mailing, your current passport is canceled when you submit, and anyone with imminent travel should still go in person.
