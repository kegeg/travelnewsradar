The [Gordie Howe International Bridge](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/) opened to vehicle traffic as early as **noon EDT on July 27th, 2026**, adding a new Canada-US land crossing between Windsor, Ontario, and Detroit, Michigan. It is the first new fixed link across that stretch of the Detroit River in nearly a century, with a direct highway-to-highway connection and modern ports of entry on both sides.

## What opened, and how you get there

Canada and Michigan [set the July 27th opening](https://www.canada.ca/en/housing-infrastructure-communities/news/2026/07/the-gordie-howe-international-bridge-will-open-on-july-27th-strengthening-trade-connectivity-and-economic-development.html) after agreeing on toll governance. The crossing is a six-lane cable-stayed bridge with a main span of about **853 metres** (about **2,800 feet**), tying into **Highway 401** on the Canadian side and **Interstate 75** on the US side.

The ports of entry are built for volume. The Canadian side has **24** primary inspection lanes and **16** toll booths; US-side coverage of the opening puts primary inspection at **36** lanes. The bridge itself is meant to run **24/7** once open.

The multi-use path for pedestrians and cyclists does **not** open with cars. The [bridge authority](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/) has that free path opening **August 5th, 2026**.

## Tolls and Breakaway

Tolls for travel in **both** directions are collected on the **Canadian** side. You do not need a Breakaway account to cross. Cash, debit/credit, and digital wallets work at the toll plaza; cash is for attended lanes.

Official [toll rates](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/) for a typical personal vehicle under **2.3 metres / 7.5 feet** high:

| Payment | CAD | USD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard personal vehicle | **$8.00** | **$5.75** |
| Breakaway personal vehicle | **$6.00** | **$4.35** |
| Personal vehicle + trailer (standard) | **$8.00 + $8.00** | **$5.75 + $5.75** |
| Personal vehicle + trailer (Breakaway) | **$6.00 + $6.00** | **$4.35 + $4.35** |

Taller vehicles, buses, RVs, and commercial trucks pay **per axle** ($12 CAD / $8.75 USD standard; Breakaway $9.60 CAD / $6.90 USD). If you are paying by debit or credit at the lane, the Canadian rate applies and your bank’s exchange rate kicks in. Cash and Breakaway accounts can be set in CAD or USD.

[Breakaway](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/toll/faq/) is the electronic discount program: free tags, prepaid account, up to **25%** off, and you can register online before you travel. After opening, personal accounts need a **$50** starting deposit to order tags. If you only cross once in a while, paying at the booth is fine. If you cross this corridor a lot, the cheaper Breakaway rate is usually worth setting up.

## Documents you still need

The bridge authority points people to [CBSA](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/) and [CBP](https://www.cbp.gov/) for the accepted list. For a standard Canada-US car trip, that usually means a **passport**, **passport card**, **NEXUS**, or (where issued) an **enhanced driver’s license**. A regular state license alone will not get you through.

If you only walk partway on the multi-use path once it opens and turn back without entering the US, you still pass the Canadian port of entry going out and coming back, so bring ID. There is no mid-bridge car turnaround if you change your mind after you commit to the crossing.

Same old border rules apply on goods: do not bring cannabis across either way, and check firearms and other restricted items before you pack the trunk.

## What this means for travelers

If you are driving between Ontario and Michigan, you now have a third major Windsor-Detroit choice alongside the Ambassador Bridge and the Detroit-Windsor Tunnel. The highway-to-highway link (401 to I-75) is cleaner than the older downtown approaches if you are towing or trying to keep a schedule. Opening day and the first busy weekends can still be slow.

Caroline is from Michigan, so we have driven this border a couple of times already, and we are planning to cross again in August. We will just take whichever crossing looks faster that day.

## What to do before you cross

1. Confirm the bridge is open for your direction and vehicle type on the [official site](https://gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/), especially in the first days after opening.
2. Pack border documents for everyone in the car. Kids need their own acceptable ID.
3. Decide whether a one-off cash/card toll is enough, or whether [Breakaway](https://account.gordiehoweinternationalbridge.com/) is worth it for repeat trips.
4. Check live border wait tools once they are posted for this crossing, and keep Ambassador / tunnel as backups.
5. If you want to walk or bike across, wait for **August 5th** and still bring travel documents.

## Bottom line

Windsor-Detroit finally has a new bridge. Tolls for a regular car start at about **$5.75 USD / $8 CAD**, Breakaway is cheaper if you cross often, and the document rules are the same as any other Canada-US land border. Check wait times on the day and pick the crossing that looks fastest.
