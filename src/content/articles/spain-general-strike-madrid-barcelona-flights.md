Spain's largest unions, CCOO and UGT, called a 24-hour general strike for **Wednesday, November 11th, 2026**, from midnight to midnight local time. The Transport Ministry has set airport-by-airport minimum flights, including **57%** of international flights at Madrid-Barajas and **56%** at Barcelona-El Prat. If you fly those airports Wednesday, check your airline.

Another Spain airport labor story after [Barcelona's Groundforce walkout](/news/barcelona-el-prat-groundforce-strike/) and [Palma's Swissport strike days](/news/palma-airport-swissport-strikes/). This one is a nationwide general strike.

## What the ministry set

[Spain's Transport Ministry](https://www.transportes.gob.es/aviacion-civil/informacion-util-al-pasajero/huelga-servicios-minimos) published a [minimum-service resolution](https://www.transportes.gob.es/recursos_mfom/paginabasica/recursos/53_26-11-11-resolucion_huelga_general_vi.pdf) for the strike, signed November 6th. It covers Wednesday from **00:00 to 24:00** local. Spain is six hours ahead of US Eastern, so that window is **6:00 p.m. Tuesday through 6:00 p.m. Wednesday** Eastern.

The ministry counts more than **4,600** takeoffs and landings programmed at Spanish airports that day, with close to **780,000** seats. Based on expected load factors, it puts more than **663,000** passengers in the potential impact window.

The order sets a share of each airline's scheduled flights on each route, airport by airport, then rounds up to a whole flight. Airports stay open. Those percentages are the **minimum** share that must keep operating. They do not identify which individual flights will delay or cancel. Airlines can still cancel more.

| Airport | Islands, Ceuta, Melilla, and public-service routes | International and long domestic | Short mainland domestic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Madrid-Barajas | 78% | 57% | 32% |
| Barcelona-El Prat | 79% | 56% | 34% |
| Málaga | 75% | 57% | 32% |
| Palma de Mallorca | 75% | 53% | n/a |
| Alicante | 74% | 54% | 31% |
| Valencia | 75% | 59% | 32% |

Island and public-service routes get the highest floor. Short mainland hops where public transport takes less than **5 hours** get the lowest. International flights, and mainland hops where public transport would take **5 hours or more**, get a middle share.

If a route links two airports on that list, the **higher** of the two percentages applies. Flights that took off before the strike and land during it stay protected.

[CCOO](https://www.ccoo.es/noticia:769574--convocamos_huelga_general_para_el_11_de_noviembre) and [UGT](https://www.ugt.es/huelga-general-11-noviembre) called the walkout over wages, housing costs, and prices. Other unions, including CGT, joined the same-day call.

## Airline change windows

No public cancellation list is out yet. Two airlines have posted traveler options.

[Iberia](https://www.iberia.com/es/latest-update-of-our-flights/) says some of its Wednesday flights may be affected. For **Iberia, Iberia Express, and Air Nostrum** trips scheduled on November 11th, including canceled flights, you can change to fly by **November 26th**, or request a refund as a voucher or to the original payment method. If you booked on iberia.com, in the app, or through the call center, use manage booking. If you booked through an agency, contact the agency. Iberia also tells passengers leaving a Spanish airport Wednesday to arrive earlier than usual, complete online check-in, and prefer a cabin bag where the fare allows it.

[American Airlines](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) is waiving change fees for American flights to, from, or through **Madrid (MAD)** or **Barcelona (BCN)** on November 11th, if you bought the ticket by **November 6th**. You can rebook travel between **November 8th and 18th**, in the same cabin and the same origin and destination cities. Changes have to be booked by November 11th. Basic Economy is included. A fare difference may still apply. Any refund depends on a separately applicable rule, or on American later canceling the flight.

If your ticket is on another airline, use that airline's app. Other carriers may not match those windows.

## Getting to the airport

[Renfe](https://grupo.renfe.com/es/es/sala-de-prensa/noticias/2026/11/establecidos-los-servicios-minimos-para-la-huelga-general-convocada-el-11n) has its own Wednesday minimums: **75%** of Cercanías trains at peak hours (**6:00-9:00 a.m.**, **1:30-3:30 p.m.**, and **6:30-8:30 p.m.** local) and **50%** the rest of the day, **65%** of Media Distancia, and **73%** of AVE and long-distance trains. Build extra time into the airport run, especially in Madrid and Barcelona.

A separate Renfe drivers' strike is also set for **Tuesday, November 10th**, and for slots on **Thursday, November 12th**. Rail can be disrupted Tuesday through Thursday.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked into or out of **Madrid-Barajas** or **Barcelona-El Prat** on Wednesday, those airports are common gateways for US and Canadian travelers. International flights have a higher protected share than short mainland hops, but a pulled long-haul still means using the airline's rebooking path. Confirm the live booking even if the route type looks well protected.

If you fly **Málaga, Palma, Alicante, or Valencia** on Wednesday, the same order applies with the shares in the table.

If you fly Thursday or later, check anyway if you were rebooked off a Wednesday flight.

If you need wheelchair or other reduced-mobility assistance at the airport, the ministry sets that staff floor at **75%** of the scheduled daily roster. Ask the airline to confirm the assistance booking.

## What to do

1. Check **your flight** in the airline app or manage booking. [Iberia](https://www.iberia.com/es/latest-update-of-our-flights/) and [American](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) have published change windows. [Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/flight-info.html) posts airport flight information.
2. If the airline cancels your flight, use its rebooking or refund path first, including any partner flight it assigns. Wait for that before you buy a seat on another carrier, or the replacement fare may not be reimbursed.
3. For flights covered by [EU air passenger rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/passenger-rights/air/index_en.htm), you can choose reimbursement or re-routing, plus care (meals, and a hotel if you are stuck overnight). Cash compensation is a separate claim and is case-specific. Airlines often argue that a general strike outside their own workforce is an extraordinary circumstance.

Keep receipts if you are stranded. Card flight-delay insurance on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the delay meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Spain's general strike runs all day Wednesday, November 11th. Madrid-Barajas has a 57% minimum service level for international flights and Barcelona-El Prat 56%. Check your airline. Iberia Group and American have posted change options for that day's tickets.
