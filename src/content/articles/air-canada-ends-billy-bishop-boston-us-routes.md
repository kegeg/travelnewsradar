Air Canada is dropping its downtown Toronto Billy Bishop (YTZ) to Boston (BOS) nonstop from **December 1st, 2026**, according to current schedule filings. Four other US routes are also gone from the summer 2027 timetable. Toronto Pearson-Charleston is one of them, and it is Air Canada's only Charleston nonstop in those filings.

Porter also uses Billy Bishop for US flying, including [twice-daily Chicago O'Hare flights](/news/porter-billy-bishop-chicago-ohare/).

## The five dropped routes

The Boston service [launched July 1st, 2026](https://www.aircanada.com/media/air-canada-to-launch-us-flights-from-toronto-island-in-a-major-expansion-at-billy-bishop-toronto-city-airport/) as three daily flights on a 78-seat Dash 8-400. Air Canada's October 2025 Billy Bishop expansion also added New York LaGuardia, Chicago O'Hare, and Washington Dulles. Air Canada's [current Toronto-US page](https://www.aircanada.com/en-ca/flights-from-toronto-to-united-states) still lists New York, Washington, and Chicago from Billy Bishop, and no longer lists Boston.

Recheck [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) for your dates. The live Toronto-Boston page currently shows Billy Bishop seats through late November, then Pearson.

Air Canada has not posted a newsroom release about the cuts. The airline said it reviews the schedule so capacity matches demand and travel patterns, and called this a commercial decision to adjust some routes next summer. The Boston drop is in December. The other four are summer 2027 returns that are now gone.

| Route | What the schedule shows now |
| --- | --- |
| Billy Bishop (YTZ)-Boston (BOS) | Canceled from December 1st, 2026 |
| Montreal (YUL)-Seattle (SEA) | Gone from summer 2027 |
| Vancouver (YVR)-Nashville (BNA) | Gone from summer 2027 |
| Vancouver (YVR)-Raleigh-Durham (RDU) | Gone from summer 2027 |
| Toronto Pearson (YYZ)-Charleston (CHS) | Gone from summer 2027 |

Air Canada said it will keep flying Boston from Toronto Pearson, and keep Nashville, Raleigh-Durham, and Seattle from other Canadian airports. Current schedules still show Air Canada Boston from Montreal and Halifax as well.

## New US routes still on the board

On **October 16th, 2026**, Air Canada and United [announced](https://www.aircanada.com/media/air-canada-expands-canada-us-connectivity-with-new-summer-2027-routes-increasing-options-for-travellers-under-its-transborder-joint-business-agreement-with-united-airlines/) three Air Canada-operated summer 2027 adds. Those dates are still what Air Canada has published. They do not replace the five dropped routes.

| Route | Published season |
| --- | --- |
| Toronto-Louisville | April 28th through October 31st, 2027 |
| Montreal-Indianapolis | June 1st through September 30th, 2027 |
| Edmonton-Newark | June 1st through October 17th, 2027 |

## What this means for travelers

If you hold an Air Canada ticket on Billy Bishop-Boston **on or after December 1st, 2026**, open the booking. Use Air Canada's rebooking path first rather than buying another ticket on your own. If the new routing does not work, Air Canada's [refund and cancellation policy](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/fly/customer-support/cancellation-refund.html) covers the unused portion when the airline canceled the flight.

If you still want a downtown Toronto-Boston nonstop after that, [Porter still sells Billy Bishop-Boston](https://www.flyporter.com/en_ca/flights-from-toronto-to-boston) into 2027. Pearson remains Air Canada's Boston airport for Toronto.

If you wanted Montreal-Seattle, Vancouver-Nashville, Vancouver-Raleigh-Durham, or Toronto Pearson-Charleston as an Air Canada nonstop next summer, those routes are not in the current timetable. Nashville, Raleigh-Durham, and Seattle remain from other Canadian airports. Charleston has no Air Canada replacement in the current filings.

US and Canadian entry rules do not change because of these route changes.

## What to do now

1. If your Air Canada ticket shows Billy Bishop-Boston on or after December 1st, retrieve it in [Air Canada Trips](https://www.aircanada.com/home/ca/en/aco/trips) and take Air Canada's rebooking or refund path.
2. If you booked through an agent or another airline's ticket, contact the ticket issuer. Air Canada cannot always change a codeshare-issued booking.
3. If you are only shopping dates, search [aircanada.com](https://www.aircanada.com/) for the airport you will actually use, or [flyporter.com](https://www.flyporter.com/en_ca/flights-from-toronto-to-boston) for downtown Boston.

## Bottom line

Air Canada is dropping Billy Bishop-Boston from **December 1st, 2026**, in current filings, plus four summer 2027 US routes, including Toronto Pearson-Charleston. Ticketed travelers should use Air Canada's rebooking or refund options. Porter still flies downtown Boston. Pearson, Montreal, and Halifax still have Air Canada Boston. Recheck the live booking for your dates.
