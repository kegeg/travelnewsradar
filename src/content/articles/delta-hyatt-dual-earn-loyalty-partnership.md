[Delta and Hyatt announced](https://news.delta.com/delta-and-hyatt-introduce-new-strategic-relationship) a new exclusive SkyMiles and World of Hyatt partnership on September 9th, 2026. Dual Earn for eligible elites is set to start in the coming months: miles on qualifying Hyatt stays, and World of Hyatt points on qualifying Delta fares. Earn rates, enrollment steps, and exact elite tiers are still unpublished.

If you still use Hyatt with American Airlines, [link those accounts by 11:59:59 p.m. CT on November 15th, 2026](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/AirlineUpdate) to keep this earning year's remaining awards.

## What Dual Earn includes

Both companies describe Dual Earn as an elite benefit at launch, with more details later.

| Who | What they earn at launch |
| --- | --- |
| Delta Medallion members | SkyMiles on spend for qualifying stays at participating Hyatt properties, plus certain other World of Hyatt benefits |
| World of Hyatt elites | World of Hyatt points on qualifying Delta airfare in qualifying fare classes, plus certain other SkyMiles benefits |
| Everyone else | No Dual Earn details yet. Free [SkyMiles](https://www.delta.com/us/en/skymiles) and [World of Hyatt](https://www.hyatt.com/world-of-hyatt) enrollment is still available |

Once it is live, that is two currencies on the same qualifying flight or stay. The companies have not published a points-per-dollar table, launch date, or enrollment steps. Watch [delta.com](https://www.delta.com/) and [hyatt.com](https://www.hyatt.com/) for those rules before you change how you book.

## Hyatt's American Airlines wind-down

The same day, Hyatt said it and American have mutually decided not to continue their enhanced relationship. For the rest of each program's current earning year, members still earn, select, and redeem eligible benefits with no interruption. Linking by November 15th is what keeps Loyalty Point Rewards through February 28th, 2027 and Milestone Rewards through December 31st, 2026.

| Item | Deadline / window |
| --- | --- |
| Link AAdvantage and World of Hyatt accounts | **11:59:59 p.m. CT on November 15th, 2026** |
| AAdvantage members: World of Hyatt awards via Loyalty Point Rewards | Through **February 28th, 2027** if linked by November 15th |
| World of Hyatt members: AAdvantage awards via Milestone Rewards | Through **December 31st, 2026** if linked by November 15th |
| 500 AAdvantage miles instead of Hyatt points | Still available after the broader relationship ends, by giving your AAdvantage number at check-in on eligible stays |

The older American dual-earn already ended on December 31st, 2024, per Hyatt's [October 2024 update](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/aaevolution). What is winding down now is the remaining linked awards.

## What this means for travelers

If you already hold Delta Medallion and World of Hyatt elite status, Dual Earn can add a second currency on trips you were going to take anyway. You cannot price that yet. Until Hyatt and Delta publish rates, [value World of Hyatt points and SkyMiles the way you already do](https://flytrippers.com/how-much-are-points-worth-points-valuations-in-canada/), and wait.

If you use Hyatt with American, November 15th is the date that matters. Link the accounts if you still want this year's Loyalty Point Rewards or Milestone Rewards. After those windows, the 500-mile check-in option can continue.

If you do not fly Delta and do not stay at Hyatt, nothing in your trip changes. Hyatt's [July 2026 Aeroplan collaboration](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/aeroplan) is a separate program, and it is unchanged.

Caroline and I stay at Hyatts a lot, including 11 cheap cash nights in Brazil last year so she could bank Globalist nights. Dual Earn is not live, and the rates are not out, so it does not change how we book a Hyatt this week.

## What to do

1. If you use AAdvantage and World of Hyatt, link the accounts before **11:59:59 p.m. CT on November 15th, 2026**. Hyatt's [relationship update](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/AirlineUpdate) lists those windows.
2. If you want Dual Earn later, keep free SkyMiles and World of Hyatt memberships. Wait for Delta and Hyatt to publish earn rates and a linking flow. Recheck [Delta's announcement](https://news.delta.com/delta-and-hyatt-introduce-new-strategic-relationship) and hyatt.com before you change how you book around it.
