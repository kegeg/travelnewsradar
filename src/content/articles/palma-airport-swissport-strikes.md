Another Europe labor watch after [easyJet France’s summer strike notice](/news/easyjet-france-cabin-crew-strike-notice/). At Palma de Mallorca Airport (PMI), staff at **Swissport** are staging **partial strikes** on **July 28th, August 1st, August 4th, and August 8th, 2026**. Stoppages run **07:00–12:00** and **19:00–22:00** local time on each of those days.

Swissport is a **ground-handling company**. [Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/airlines/operating-at-aena/commercial-and-operational-aspects/handling-and-self-assistance.html) runs Spanish airports and licenses handlers; Swissport is one of the firms airlines hire for check-in, boarding, bags, and ramp work. Only airlines that use Swissport at Palma sit in the direct line of fire. The airport stays open.

PMI is already peak-summer busy, and Mallorca is in the path of totality for the **August 12th, 2026** total solar eclipse, which is pulling extra visitors into the island that week.

## What is on, and what was called off

[CCOO’s aviation section](https://aereo.fsc.ccoo.es/noticia:764899--CCOO_convoca_cuatro_jornadas_de_huelga_parcial_en_Swissport_Palma_para_reclamar_mejoras_en_prevencion_organizacion_y_condiciones_laborales&opc_id=85218c6354c7ad262b6b1b98e6713175) called the four partial strike days for Swissport’s Palma handling staff. Mediation at the Balearic arbitration tribunal (TAMIB) did not produce firm commitments, so those dates stayed on. Union demands focus on heat protection, staffing, work organization, and pay alignment with the sector handling agreement. CCOO puts Swissport Palma’s handling workforce around **600** people.

## Who is most affected

Swissport does not handle every airline at PMI. CCOO lists clients including **TUI, Condor, TAP Air Portugal, Air Canada, Etihad Airways, and DAT**.

If your ticket is on one of those carriers into or out of Palma on a strike day, the morning and evening windows are when bag drop, boarding, and reclaim are most likely to slow down. Spanish minimum-service rules usually keep flights moving, but turnarounds can still slip.

Other carriers at PMI use different handlers. Ryanair, easyJet, and Jet2 are commonly cited in that group. Their flights are not the direct target of this Swissport walkout. When Swissport flights run late, the apron and terminal can still feel crowded, so a status check on travel day is still worth it.

## Why eclipse week makes PMI tighter

Mallorca is inside the path of totality for the August 12th eclipse. In Palma, totality is expected around **8:31 p.m. local time** for about **one minute and 36 seconds**, with the sun very low on the western horizon. Partial phases start earlier in the evening.

That is already a big travel draw on top of normal August tourism. Swissport strike days on **August 1st, 4th, and 8th** sit in the run-up. Even if your airline uses another handler, plan for a fuller airport and less spare capacity if something slips.

## What this means for travelers

If you fly a Swissport-handled airline on those four dates, treat the morning and evening windows as higher-risk periods for bags and boarding. Leave buffer for same-day connections, transfers, and hotel check-in.

If you fly Ryanair or another non-Swissport carrier at PMI, you should still watch status, because late July and early August at Palma do not leave much slack.

We are planning to go to Mallorca for that eclipse. We're flying Ryanair from Sofia into Palma on **August 9th** and leaving for Barcelona on **August 13th**. So we should clear those airport disturbances, but we're keeping an eye on the situation. We booked a nice Hyatt on points about a year ago, before rates jumped and while rooms were still available.

## What to do now

1. Confirm whether **your airline** uses Swissport at Palma, then match your dates to July 28th and August 1st, 4th, and 8th.
2. If you are on a Swissport-handled carrier, prefer flights outside **07:00–12:00** and **19:00–22:00** local when you can, or leave extra time for bags.
3. Travel light if your fare allows it. Bag systems usually feel ground-handling pressure first.
4. Turn on airline alerts and check status before you leave for the airport on strike days.

## Bottom line

Swissport ground handlers at Palma are staging partial morning and evening strikes on July 28th and August 1st, 4th, and 8th. Risk is highest for Swissport’s airline clients in those windows, during peak summer and with eclipse traffic building toward August 12th. Check who handles your flight, and leave buffer.
