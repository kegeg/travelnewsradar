Two Houthi ballistic missiles headed toward the Mecca and Medina regions were intercepted on Saturday, October 24th, 2026. Saudi Civil Defense teams responded to falling debris in Mecca, Jeddah, Taif, and Rabigh. Houthis claimed hits on three Saudi airports the same day, which Saudi officials have not confirmed. Check your booking before you leave for the airport.

This follows [King Khalid staying closed after the October 10th attack](/news/riyadh-airport-closed-after-saturday-attack/) and the earlier [Aden airport diversion to Jeddah](/news/aden-airport-attack-cairo-flight-diverted-jeddah/).

## What Saudi officials confirmed

The [General Directorate of Civil Defense](https://spa.gov.sa/en/N2709301) said teams in the Mecca and Medina regions responded Saturday to falling debris after two ballistic missiles launched by Yemen's Houthis toward those regions were intercepted. Parts of the intercepted missiles fell in Wadi Al-Mughammas in Mecca, a valley east of the city near Arafat. Interception debris also fell in the Al-Nuzha neighborhood in Jeddah, Wadi Jalil in Taif, and Masturah in Rabigh. The statement did not list casualties.

Earlier Saturday, the Saudi-led coalition said air defenses [intercepted a ballistic missile toward Riyadh](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2708936), [two ballistic missiles toward Taif and Yanbu](https://spa.gov.sa/en/N2709057), and [a drone in the Eastern Region](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2708901).

Yemen has been at war since 2014. The Houthis control much of the country's northwest and have been firing missiles and drones at Saudi airports, energy sites, and shipping. Jeddah is the kingdom's main west-coast international airport and the usual air gateway for Mecca. Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport has been shut since the October 10th attack.

## What Houthis claimed

Houthi forces [said](https://www.saba.ye/en/news3817654.htm) they carried out seven operations with ballistic missiles and drones. They listed King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh, in two phases; an Aramco facility in Yanbu; the Rabigh oil refinery; crude-oil pumping stations and the East-West pipeline; King Fahd International Airport in Dammam; Najran Airport; and an air base in Khamis Mushait. They claimed the Dammam strike disrupted air traffic.

Saudi officials have not confirmed that those claimed airport or oil-site strikes hit their targets. Houthis later [denied](https://www.saba.ye/en/news3817842.htm) targeting Mecca and Medina, and said their operations target oil facilities and military bases.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked into or out of Jeddah, Taif, Medina, Riyadh, Dammam, or Najran this weekend, open the reservation before you go. Jeddah's airport has not posted a closure. For Jeddah and Mecca, officials confirmed falling debris after intercepts.

If the airline canceled your flight, use its rebooking path first (app, manage booking, phone, or a partner flight it assigns). Wait for that offer before you buy a second ticket on another carrier.

King Khalid has been closed since October 10th. On Saturday, the airport [said on social media](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/riyadh-airport-announces-new-terminal-arrangements-for-domestic-international-flights/4099869) that from Sunday, October 25th, Terminal 1 is for domestic flights and Terminal 2 is for international flights operated by national carriers. Confirm your flight with the airline before you go. The notice named national carriers only.

[Delta delayed](https://news.delta.com/delta-delay-planned-flight-atlanta-riyadh-saudi-arabia-until-march-2027) its planned Atlanta-Riyadh launch until March 2027. If you hold one of those tickets, use [Delta's Riyadh waiver](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/other-alerts/riyadh-security-situation): travel dates October 23rd, 2026, through March 26th, 2027, on tickets issued on or before October 12th. Same-cabin changes through May 31st, 2027, can waive the fare difference, or you can cancel for a refund or eCredit.

If you are already in Jeddah, Mecca, or Medina, follow Civil Defense alerts. The UK says falling debris from interceptions is the main risk, and if you are advised to take shelter, you are safest inside a building.

If you are still deciding whether to go, [Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/saudi-arabia) advises against non-essential travel to Saudi Arabia (last updated October 22nd, 2026). That page still says King Khalid is currently closed, and it tells Canadians to avoid all travel to King Khalid and to King Fahd International Airport in Dammam, plus Asir, Jazan, Najran, and within 30 km (about 19 miles) of the Yemen border. The [US](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html) is at Level 3, Reconsider Travel, in an advisory issued October 23rd, 2026, with Do Not Travel along the Yemen border. The same day, it ordered non-emergency US government employees and their families to leave. The [UK](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/saudi-arabia), still current October 25th and last updated October 21st, advises against all but essential travel to Saudi Arabia, and against all travel within 10 km (about 6 miles) of the Yemen border. It says King Khalid continues to be targeted, and that further airport attacks, delays, and cancellations are very likely. If you are a British national who needs to leave, the UK says to consider a land route to a neighboring country and fly from there, and to check that country's entry rules.

## What to do now

1. If you have booked a Saudi flight this weekend, open the reservation in the airline app before you leave for any airport.
2. If the flight is canceled, take the airline's rebooking or refund path first.
3. Recheck the [Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/saudi-arabia), [US](https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/saudi-arabia.html), and [UK](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/saudi-arabia) pages the same day you travel.
4. If you are already in western Saudi Arabia, follow Civil Defense alerts and stay inside if advised to take shelter.
5. If you hold a delayed Delta Atlanta-Riyadh ticket, use [My Trips](https://www.delta.com/) and the Riyadh waiver before you pay a change fee.

If a cancellation leaves you overnight, a card with flight-delay insurance can still help with hotel and meal costs. For the practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).
