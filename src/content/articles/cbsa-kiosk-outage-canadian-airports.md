The Canada Border Services Agency's [primary inspection kiosks](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/border-tech-frontiere/pik-bip-eng.html) went down Friday afternoon, **September 11th, 2026**, at **11 Canadian international airports**, including Toronto Pearson and Montreal-Trudeau, and officers processed arriving travelers by hand. CBSA said the outage was resolved Friday evening, around **9:30 p.m.** Eastern, so if you land today, the kiosks should be working again.

CBSA told travelers to have documents ready while systems were down. A spokesperson said there is no indication of a cyberattack, and that the agency is investigating the cause. Toronto Pearson confirmed the kiosks in **Terminals 1 and 3** were affected and that customs waits could run longer than usual.

## Which airports were affected

CBSA said kiosks were hit at:

- Toronto Pearson (YYZ), Terminals 1 and 3
- Billy Bishop Toronto City (YTZ)
- Hamilton (YHM)
- Montreal-Trudeau (YUL)
- Quebec City (YQB)
- Ottawa (YOW)
- Halifax (YHZ)
- Winnipeg (YWG)
- Calgary (YYC)
- Edmonton (YEG)
- Vancouver (YVR)

Those are the airports where CBSA kiosks are installed. Domestic flights inside Canada do not go through CBSA.

## What this means for travelers

If you are arriving in Canada today or later this weekend, the kiosks are back online and no new disruption has been announced. A leftover line is still possible after Friday's manual processing, so leave the same customs time you already planned.

US visitors and Canadians coming home use the same kiosk-or-officer path on a US or other international arrival. You scan a passport or Canadian permanent resident card, take a photo, answer the declaration questions, then take a receipt to an officer. Up to **five** people can share one kiosk. If the machines fail, [CBSA's kiosk how-to](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/services/border-tech-frontiere/how-comment-eng.html) says staff will send you to an officer.

Friday's outage meant slower customs, with officers still clearing people without the machines. There is no official wait-time figure for Friday night.

CBSA had another 11-airport kiosk outage in **May 2026**. Friday's cause is still under investigation.

## What to do

1. If you land this weekend, have your passport ready and use the kiosks.
2. You can submit an [Advance Declaration](https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/services/arrivecan/declaration.html) in ArriveCAN up to **72 hours** before you fly into a participating airport, then confirm it at the kiosk. That only speeds you up when the kiosks are working.
3. If the kiosks fail again, follow staff to an officer.

## Bottom line

CBSA's airport kiosks were down Friday afternoon and evening at 11 airports, then came back around 9:30 p.m. For a trip landing now, the kiosks should be working. A leftover customs line is still possible.
