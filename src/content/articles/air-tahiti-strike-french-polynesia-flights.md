Air Tahiti cabin crew and some ground staff started walking out on **Thursday, October 22nd, 2026**. Fueling staff at Tahiti-Faa'a are walking out at the same time, and island hops can cancel in the hour before departure. If you have booked on Air Tahiti, check the reservation before you go to the airport.

Another labor disruption after [easyJet Portugal cabin crew walked out](/news/easyjet-portugal-cabin-crew-strike/). This one is the island hops inside French Polynesia.

## Who is walking out

Air Tahiti is French Polynesia's inter-island airline, based at Tahiti-Faa'a (PPT). It flies the hops to places such as Bora Bora, Moorea, Raiatea, and Huahine. Air Tahiti Nui is the separate long-haul airline into Papeete.

[Air Tahiti's October 22nd notice](https://www.airtahiti.pf/fr/info-greve) says the walkout began at midnight Thursday after talks with O OE TO OE RIMA, CSIP, and SPENCAT did not produce an agreement. It covers some ground staff and cabin crew. As of Thursday, the company said about **40** of its roughly **1,600** employees were taking part, about half of them cabin crew.

A second walkout is underway at SOMSTAT, the company that fuels aircraft at Tahiti-Faa'a. Air Tahiti says a fueling stoppage can cancel a flight even when the crew is there.

Cabin crew only have to present one hour before departure, so some cancellations will only be known then. If you have not had an SMS or email, Air Tahiti still wants you at check-in on the original time. The company said Friday's flying was still being adjusted as of Thursday. There is no published end date.

Air Tahiti has not posted a list of protected flights. CSIP says no minimum-service agreement was negotiated.

The walkout overlaps school holidays and the Hawaiki Nui Va'a canoe race, scheduled October 28th through 31st.

## What this means for travelers

If you have booked **on Air Tahiti** between the islands, you are the group that can get hit. Same-day connections off a long-haul into Papeete can leave you in Tahiti overnight if the island hop cancels after you land.

[Air Tahiti Nui puts its TN code](https://www.airtahitinui.com/en/news/corporate/air-tahiti-nui-and-air-tahiti-confirm-their-codeshare-agreement) on some Air Tahiti-operated Bora Bora and Rarotonga flights. Check the confirmation for "operated by Air Tahiti." The long-haul into PPT is a different flight.

If the ticket and the operating flight are both on another airline, this Air Tahiti staff walkout does not apply to you. SOMSTAT fuels planes at Faa'a for more than Air Tahiti. The airline has not posted a list of other carriers' cancellations.

[Air Tahiti's passenger page](https://www.airtahiti.pf/fr/greve-informations-clients) says that if it cancels because of its own staff strike and you are not a resident of that island, it covers a hotel up to a set amount at properties it chooses. If the cancel is because SOMSTAT did not fuel the plane, staff will help you look for a room, and you pay.

## Ticket changes

For any ticket, including Passes, with at least one Air Tahiti flight between Thursday, October 22nd, and Tuesday, October 27th:

- Change without a fee, including a different island. A fare difference can still apply.
- Cancel without a fee for a credit valid one year.

If your ticket has no Air Tahiti flight in that dated window, recheck the live notice before you pay a fee. The company's press notice already says it will waive change and cancel fees for the duration of the walkout, which can last past the 27th.

## What to do now

1. Open [Manage my booking](https://www.airtahiti.com/en/manage-booking) or the Air Tahiti app, and check [today's arrivals and departures](https://www.airtahiti.com/en/arrivals-departures).
2. If Air Tahiti already canceled the flight, use that rebooking or credit path first. Third-party and package bookings go back to the seller. Call [+689 40 86 42 42](tel:+68940864242) or the agency that issued the ticket.
3. Put a reachable phone and email on the reservation. Ignore a first SMS that assigns **VT1001 through VT1019**. That is a placeholder so the booking stays open. A later message with a normal flight number is the real one.
4. If you have no notification, still go to check-in at the original time.

Keep receipts if you get stuck. Card flight-delay insurance on many Canadian and US travel cards can help when the delay meets that card's terms. For the broader checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Air Tahiti staff and Faa'a fueling staff started walking out Thursday, and island hops can cancel close to departure. Fee-free changes, including a different island, are posted for tickets that include a flight from October 22nd through 27th. A cancel becomes a one-year credit.
