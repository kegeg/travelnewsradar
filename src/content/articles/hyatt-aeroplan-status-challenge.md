World of Hyatt Explorist and Globalist members can now register for a 90-day Aeroplan Elite Status Challenge. After you [link the accounts](https://www.hyatt.com/profile/alliances/aeroplan?openLinkModal=true), an Explorist who earns 8,000 Status Qualifying Credits (SQC) on eligible Air Canada flying in that window gets Aeroplan 25K status, and a Globalist can go as high as Aeroplan 75K.

Linked members can also register for a stay bonus that pays Aeroplan points on Hyatt stays. Another Hyatt story already on the site is the [Delta Dual Earn partnership](/news/delta-hyatt-dual-earn-loyalty-partnership/).

Hyatt [said in July](https://newsroom.hyatt.com/aeroplan) that this Aeroplan-side challenge was coming later in 2026. [Air Canada's challenge page](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/status-challenge.html) is live.

## How the Aeroplan challenge works

You register with the Register button on Hyatt's [Aeroplan Elite Status Challenge offer page](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/offers/air-canada-status-challenge.html) while logged into World of Hyatt. Processing can take up to 48 hours. The 90 days start when Aeroplan records the registration, not when you click. A confirmation email lists your start and end dates.

Your World of Hyatt status on the day you register sets the challenge. If you register as an Explorist and later become a Globalist, you stay in the Explorist challenge for the full 90 days.

| World of Hyatt status at registration | SQC in 90 days | Aeroplan status | Usual calendar-year SQC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explorist | 8,000 | 25K | 25,000 |
| Globalist | 8,000 | 25K | 25,000 |
| Globalist | 12,000 | 35K | 35,000 |
| Globalist | 17,000 | 50K | 50,000 |
| Globalist | 25,000 | 75K | 75,000 |

Usual-year figures are from [Aeroplan's status page](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/aeroplan/status.html). Super Elite is not part of this challenge.

You get one challenge per calendar year. If you do not finish, you can try again next year, and only after the 90 days have ended.

Status earned through the challenge lasts until January 31st of the second calendar year after the year you registered, if you hit the required SQC in time. Air Canada's own examples: register on November 1st, 2026, and the status runs to January 31st, 2028. Register on February 1st, 2027, and it runs to January 31st, 2029.

You cannot enroll if you already hold Aeroplan status, earned outside the challenge, that is equal to or higher than the top tier this challenge would give you and that status is valid through the following calendar year. An Explorist whose 25K runs only to January 31st, 2027, can still enroll during 2026.

Discoverist members, including complimentary Discoverist from an Aeroplan premium card, are not eligible.

## What counts toward the challenge

Only eligible Air Canada activity during the 90 days counts, and flights have to depart inside that window.

- Flights operated by Air Canada, Air Canada Express, or Air Canada Rouge in Economy Standard or higher
- Star Alliance and select partner flights ticketed by Air Canada (ticket number starting with 014) in Economy Standard or higher
- Air Canada Vacations packages, which earn 1 SQC per $1 on the full package value per member, including taxes and fees, in any cabin

On Air Canada-issued tickets (number starting with 014), you get 4 SQC per $1 on Economy Flex or higher and 2 SQC per $1 on Economy Standard, counted on the base fare and carrier surcharges, not taxes, fees, and third-party charges. If another airline issued the ticket (number not starting with 014) and Air Canada operates the flight, you get 2 SQC per $1 in Economy and 4 SQC per $1 in Premium Economy or Business.

Economy Basic fares and Aeroplan flight rewards, including Points + Cash bookings, earn no SQC toward the challenge. SQC from credit cards, other non-flight activity, and flight promotions also do not count. SQC can take up to 14 days to post. After the 90 days end, Air Canada still allows 120 days for SQC from flights you actually took inside the window to credit.

## The Hyatt stay bonus

Linked members can register through November 11th, 2026, to earn 1.5 Aeroplan points per eligible US$1 spent at participating Hyatt hotels, on top of standard World of Hyatt points. Stays have to be completed after you register, with checkout no later than February 14th, 2027.

That is different from the standing choice of 500 Aeroplan points per stay instead of World of Hyatt points. During this window you keep the Hyatt points.

Register in your logged-in World of Hyatt account.

## What this means for travelers

If you already have Hyatt stays through mid-February, register for the stay bonus by November 11th.

The status challenge only helps if you have eligible paid flying in the 90 days. If your Air Canada tickets are awards, they do not count. You get one try a year, so add up the SQC on flights you will actually take before you register.

If you do not stay at Hyatt and you do not fly Air Canada, nothing in your next trip changes.

Caroline and I stay at Hyatts a lot. I also redeem Aeroplan awards, and those tickets earn no SQC here. The stay bonus is the one I would actually register for without a paid Air Canada trip on the calendar.

See [Flytrippers' current points valuations](https://flytrippers.com/how-much-are-points-worth-points-valuations-in-canada/) for World of Hyatt points and Aeroplan points.

## What to do

1. [Link](https://www.hyatt.com/profile/alliances/aeroplan?openLinkModal=true) your Aeroplan and World of Hyatt accounts if they are not already linked. First and last names have to match, and both accounts have to be in good standing.
2. If you have participating Hyatt stays through February 14th, 2027, register for the stay bonus by November 11th, 2026.
3. If you want the Aeroplan challenge, add up eligible flights first, then register on Hyatt's [challenge offer page](https://world.hyatt.com/content/gp/en/offers/air-canada-status-challenge.html). Wait for the confirmation email; it lists the start and end dates Aeroplan has already recorded.

## Bottom line

Explorist and Globalist members can register for a 90-day Aeroplan challenge at 8,000 SQC for 25K, up to 25,000 SQC for 75K if you hold Globalist. Linked members can also register by November 11th for 1.5 Aeroplan points per eligible US$1 on Hyatt stays through February 14th. Confirm both Register buttons in your World of Hyatt account.
