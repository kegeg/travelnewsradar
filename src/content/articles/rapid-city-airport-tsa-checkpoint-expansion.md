Rapid City Regional Airport (RAP) has opened an expanded TSA checkpoint. Airport officials say the new area went into use on Friday, October 16th, 2026. If you are flying out of RAP, currently posted ID, boarding pass, and liquids steps still apply. Nothing material should change in how you pack or which ID you bring.

Related TSA checkpoint reading: [TSA CT scanners](/news/tsa-ct-scanners-carry-on-bags/) and [Gateside PreCheck expansion paused](/news/tsa-gateside-precheck-expansion-paused/).

## What opened

The checkpoint is part of [Project 1](https://rapairport.com/terminal/) of the airport's terminal renovation, which also covers baggage screening and ticketing. An [August 20th, 2026 airport briefing](https://boardsandcommissions.sd.gov/bcuploads/ATTACH%206%20-%20Informational%20Item%20RAP%20Airport%20Projects%20(1).pdf) to the South Dakota Aeronautics Commission put that work at **$70.6 million**, with about 69% federal, 26% local, and 5% state funding.

Executive Director Patrick Dame said the old checkpoint is still being renovated so TSA staff can work next to the new lanes. Officials say that once the work is fully complete, there could be as many as five passenger lanes. The airport has not posted how many lanes are running today.

RAP is the main commercial airport for western South Dakota and the gateway to the Black Hills, Mount Rushmore, and Badlands National Park. It [enplaned 206,010 passengers](https://rapairport.com/record-first-half-of-2026/) from January through June 2026, a first-half record. The airport's 2025 total was **452,195** enplanements, also a record. About 65% of annual traffic moves in the summer months.

[Five airlines](https://rapairport.com/frequently-asked-questions/) operate at RAP: Allegiant, American, Delta, United, and Sun Country. Year-round nonstops include Denver, Dallas-Fort Worth, Minneapolis, and Las Vegas.

## What you need at the checkpoint

The new area is a facility expansion. The airport's currently posted TSA steps call for an [acceptable ID](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/identification) and a boarding pass, and the [3-1-1 liquids rule](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/liquids-rule) applies. RAP already has a designated TSA PreCheck lane. There is no on-site PreCheck enrollment center.

The airport still tells travelers to arrive at least two hours before a domestic departure. Ticket counters open two hours before departure and close 45 minutes before the flight.

The rest of the terminal project is still underway. A [May 2026 airport request for proposals](https://rapairport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-SecurityServicesRFPBroomFinal.pdf) said Phase 2 includes the TSA checkpoint relocation and the concourse exit lane. Ticketing and baggage work continues in the same project.

## What this means for travelers

If you are not flying RAP, this does not apply.

If you are departing Rapid City, pack to the currently posted ID, boarding pass, and liquids steps. Follow the officer at the lane. Keep the two-hour buffer the airport already publishes. Construction is still visible in the terminal.

The new checkpoint is for departing RAP passengers. You do not use it on arrival.

## What to do

1. Still plan to arrive at least two hours before a domestic RAP departure.
2. Follow the officer at your lane. If TSA PreCheck is on your boarding pass, use that lane when it is open.
3. If you are checking a bag, get to the ticket counter before it closes 45 minutes before departure.

## Bottom line

RAP's expanded TSA checkpoint is open. Posted ID, boarding pass, and liquids steps still apply. Keep the two-hour buffer, and the rest of the terminal project is still underway.
