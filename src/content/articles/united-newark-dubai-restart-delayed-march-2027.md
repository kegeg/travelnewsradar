United has pulled its Newark (EWR) to Dubai (DXB) nonstop off the winter 2026/27 schedule. Current schedule data first lists a daily restart on **March 27th, 2027**. If you hold a United ticket that still shows that city pair this winter, open the booking.

This comes from airline schedule databases. United has not posted a matching newsroom note. Recheck [united.com](https://www.united.com/) for your dates before you lock hotels or onward tickets around a restart day.

Another North American Dubai schedule story after [Air Canada’s Dubai and Tel Aviv suspension into 2027](/news/air-canada-dubai-tel-aviv-suspension/) and last week’s [Dubai and Abu Dhabi flight delays](/news/dubai-abu-dhabi-flight-delays-us-iran/).

## What the schedule shows now

United stopped operating the daily Newark-Dubai nonstop after the February 2026 Iran war began, and it has not returned. The airline later filed an **October 24th, 2026** restart. That winter start is gone from the current schedule. The same data now first shows the route on March 27th, 2027, as a daily flight. That date can still move.

United already brought Newark-Tel Aviv back on **September 8th, 2026**. It still has no Dubai flights.

[Air Canada’s Middle East travel update](https://www.aircanada.com/ca/en/aco/home/book/travel-news-and-updates/2026/middle-east-travel.html), dated August 4th, 2026, still keeps Dubai canceled until mid-January 2027. That is a different airline and a different ticket.

## What this means for travelers

If you wanted a United nonstop between Newark and Dubai this winter, that option is not in the current schedule. You can still fly from the New York area to Dubai on other airlines. Emirates currently operates Newark-Dubai via Athens, and JFK still has nonstop Dubai service. Price the full trip, including the stop and bag rules, and confirm the operating flight on [Emirates flight status](https://www.emirates.com/english/manage-booking/flight-status/) or your carrier’s app.

If you already hold a United ticket that lists Newark and Dubai on a date before March 27th, 2027, use United’s rebooking path first, before you buy a second ticket. United has dropped most change fees on standard tickets. Basic Economy and some partner tickets can still be different, so read the fare on your reservation.

United’s [travel alerts](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) page still lists Dubai under Middle East unrest flexibility, with original travel dates printed through **September 7th, 2026**. That printed window has already ended. Do not assume a current fee-free waiver.

[Global Affairs Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/united-arab-emirates) currently tells Canadians to avoid non-essential travel to the United Arab Emirates, last updated **September 9th, 2026**. The US advisory is **Level 3: Reconsider Travel** on the [State Department UAE page](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/united-arab-emirates-travel-advisory.html), listed **March 3rd, 2026**. A [US Embassy UAE update](https://ae.usembassy.gov/travel-advisory-united-arab-emirates-level-3-reconsider-travel-august-29-2026/) on **August 29th, 2026** reported no change to the Level 3 rating or risk indicators. Advisories are one planning input. Dubai International Airport itself is still open, with limited and changing service from other airlines.

## What to do now

1. Open your reservation on [united.com](https://www.united.com/) or the United app and confirm whether a United Newark-Dubai flight is still on the ticket.
2. Read United’s [travel alerts](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) page the same day. The printed Middle East flexibility window on that page ran through September 7th.
3. If you still need to travel this winter and United is not operating your dates, price another airline, including a JFK nonstop or Emirates via Athens, and confirm that flight is operating as scheduled.
4. Recheck Canada’s [UAE advice](https://travel.gc.ca/destinations/united-arab-emirates) and the [US Level 3 advisory](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/traveladvisories/traveladvisories/united-arab-emirates-travel-advisory.html) before you keep a leisure trip on the books.

## Bottom line

United’s winter schedule no longer shows a Newark-Dubai nonstop. Schedule data currently points to a daily restart on March 27th, 2027. Ticketed travelers should manage the booking on united.com, and people still shopping should use another airline or wait for dates that can still move.
