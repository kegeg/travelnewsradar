Chase's [Marriott Bonvoy Boundless apply page](https://creditcards.chase.com/travel-credit-cards/marriott-bonvoy/boundless) still shows 200,000 bonus points after you spend $3,000 on eligible purchases within 3 months of opening the card. Chase labels that welcome as this week only. The annual fee is $95.

Another Chase rewards story already on the site is the [Hyatt transfer-rate cut](/news/chase-hyatt-transfer-ratio-select-cards/).

Chase says the offer can vary by apply path, including online versus a branch. Read the 200,000-point line on the page you actually submit.

## The 200,000-point bonus

You have to put $3,000 of eligible purchases on the new Boundless card in the first 3 months. After you qualify, Chase says to allow 6 to 8 weeks for the bonus to post to your Marriott Bonvoy account. The account has to stay open and not in default at the time of fulfillment. If it is not open for at least 6 months, Marriott and Chase can take the bonus points back.

Ordinary card spend counts. Balance transfers, cash advances, cash-like transactions, crypto, person-to-person transfers, gambling, interest, fees, and the annual fee do not.

Chase does not print a calendar end date. The page banner is "OFFER ENDING SOON," and the copy is "this week only."

## Airline statement credits

New cardmembers who apply between June 4th and December 31st, 2026, are automatically registered upon card activation for up to $100 in airline statement credits through June 30th, 2027.

- Spend $250 or more directly with airlines from June 4th through December 31st, 2026, for a $50 credit.
- Spend another $250 or more from January 1st through June 30th, 2027, for a second $50 credit.

Credits can take 6 to 8 weeks. Chase can reverse them if the purchase is returned, canceled, or modified, or if you close the account within 90 days of a credit. A product change into another Chase Marriott Bonvoy card makes you ineligible for that offer.

## What the card still does after the bonus

| Benefit | Chase's published terms |
| --- | --- |
| Hotel earn | 6X at participating Marriott hotels, plus Marriott member points and Silver. Chase's published total is up to 17X |
| Everyday earn | 3X on the first $6,000 a year combined at grocery stores, gas stations, and dining, then 2X. 2X on everything else |
| Anniversary night | A Free Night Award each year, up to 35,000 points. You can combine it with Bonvoy points. Chase's [cardmember page](https://marriott.chase.com/boundless) says up to 25,000. Some hotels charge resort fees |
| Status | Automatic Silver Elite, 15 Elite Night Credits a year, 1 more Elite Night Credit per $5,000 spend, and Gold if you spend $35,000 in a calendar year |
| Foreign transaction fees | None |

Points transferred to Marriott follow Marriott's 24-month activity rule. Earning on the card counts as activity.

## Eligibility

Boundless is a US Chase card.

The card is not available if you currently hold Boundless, Bold, or the older Premier versions, or if you took a new-cardmember bonus on those Chase Marriott cards in the last 24 months.

The 200,000-point bonus is also unavailable if you currently have, or had in the last 30 days, the Marriott Bonvoy American Express Card or the old Starwood Preferred Guest Amex. The same bonus is unavailable if you were approved in the last 90 days, or took a new-cardmember or upgrade bonus in the last 24 months, on Marriott Bonvoy Business Amex, Brilliant, or Bevy, including the old SPG versions of those cards.

## What this means for travelers

If you stay at Marriott and a US Chase card is an option, 200,000 points after $3,000 is the public offer.

If you do not stay at Marriott hotels, skip it. The $95 fee and the spend only make sense if you will use the points and the anniversary night.
