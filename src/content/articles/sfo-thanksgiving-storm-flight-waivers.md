United, American, and Southwest opened fee-free change tools for eligible Wednesday, November 25th tickets to, from, or through San Francisco International Airport (SFO). As of about 11:07 p.m. Eastern Tuesday, November 24th, the FAA said SFO arrivals were delayed an average of 2 hours and 4 minutes because of wind.

This is the same check-your-booking habit as [September's Northeast storm waivers](/news/northeast-storm-weekend-flight-waivers/) and [August's Chicago storm waivers](/news/chicago-ohare-storm-ground-stops-waivers/). A separate coastal storm is also forecast for the East Coast on Thanksgiving Day.

## What happened

The [National Weather Service in the Bay Area](https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?issuedby=MTR&product=CFW&site=NWS) still had a coastal flood warning for San Francisco County and the San Francisco and San Pablo Bay shorelines through 4 p.m. Pacific Thursday, November 26th. At the San Francisco tide gauge, high tide is forecast at 8.5 feet MLLW (about 2.6 m) at 10:49 a.m. Wednesday, 2.7 feet (about 82 cm) above normal.

[SFO's November 23rd weather note](https://www.flysfo.com/flight-info/alerts-advisories/sfo-weather-preparation) said the airport staged sandbags and portable flood barriers in high-risk areas, cleared drainage, and readied backup pumps. San Francisco Mayor Daniel Lurie said SFO is no longer expecting flooding to force a ground stop, though high winds could still delay flights.

The [FAA's SFO status page](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=SFO&p=1) showed a wind-driven traffic management program delaying some arrivals an average of 2 hours and 4 minutes. Those pages are not flight-specific. The [national status board](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) listed that program through 3:59 a.m. Eastern Wednesday, and still showed a possible, then probable, SFO ground stop or delay program later Wednesday.

Separately, the [FAA's Thanksgiving travel forecast](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/trumps-transportation-department-announces-record-breaking-thanksgiving-air-travel-2026) said rain could affect flights across the Southeast, including Atlanta, on Wednesday before moving into the Mid-Atlantic later in the day, with rain and wind returning to the Northeast on Thanksgiving Day. [NOAA's Weather Prediction Center](https://www.noaa.gov/news/thanksgiving-week-weather-potent-coastal-storms-rain-and-snow-to-impact-travel) put Thursday morning travel trouble from the Carolinas to southern New England. The [National Weather Service in Boston](https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?issuedby=BOX&product=AFD&site=NWS) said much of Thanksgiving morning in that forecast area is trending dry, with rain overspreading in the afternoon and easterly gusts of 45 to 55 mph (about 72 to 89 km/h) late Thursday into Thursday night.

## What this means for travelers

If you are booked to, from, or through SFO on Wednesday, check **your flight** before you leave for the airport. Wind is already delaying arrivals, and a delayed airplane or crew can still miss a later connection.

If you have booked on United, American, or Southwest, open manage booking before you pay a change fee. American's listed SFO change deadline is November 25th.

If you are booked through Miami on Wednesday, American posted a separate November 25th waiver. If you are booked through Atlanta or other Southeast airports, the weather forecast is the reason to check status.

If you are booked through Washington, Philadelphia, New York, Newark, or Boston on Thursday, Delta and American both posted Thanksgiving Day Northeast waivers. Southwest's live advisory is still SFO on November 25th.

Canadian itineraries that connect at SFO or Miami on Wednesday, or at the Northeast hubs on Thursday, can take the same weather hit as a US domestic ticket. Waiver eligibility still depends on the operating airline and the ticket rules.

If you are still booking a trip after this week, nothing material should change.

## Airline waivers still in play

Rules differ by ticket purchase date, original travel date, allowed new dates, cabin, and city pair. Open manage booking before you pay a change fee.

| Airline | Original travel | Book/change by | New travel window |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| American | November 25th | November 25th | November 23rd through 28th |
| Southwest | November 25th | no listed book-by date | within 14 days of original date |
| United | November 25th | confirm in manage booking | through November 28th |

[American's severe weather alert](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) (current as of November 24th) covers American-operated flights in any fare class, including Basic Economy, to, from, or through SFO. Tickets must have been bought by November 22nd. You must be scheduled to travel November 25th, can travel November 23rd through 28th between the same cities, and stay in the same cabin or pay the difference. Changes must be booked by November 25th.

The same alerts page also lists a November 25th waiver for American-operated flights to, from, or through Miami (MIA). Tickets must have been bought by November 23rd. You must be scheduled to travel November 25th, can travel November 24th through 28th between the same cities, and stay in the same cabin or pay the difference. Changes must be booked by November 25th.

[Southwest's San Francisco advisory](https://www.southwest.com/travel-advisory/) covers SFO on November 25th. Eligible customers may keep the existing reservation, or rebook or travel standby within 14 days of the original travel date between the original city pairs without paying a change in airfare. If Southwest cancels or significantly delays the flight and you choose not to travel, refund rules on that page still apply.

United posted a San Francisco flooding waiver for Wednesday SFO tickets. Eligible travelers can move to a United flight through November 28th in the same cabin and cities, with San Jose (SJC) listed as an alternate, according to reports of that waiver. Confirm the November 22nd purchase cutoff, the November 28th endpoint, same-city and same-cabin rules, and the SJC option in manage booking. United's public [travel alerts page](https://www.united.com/en/us/fly/travel/trip-planning/travel-alerts.html) currently has no named SFO window.

If you have booked on another airline, check that carrier before you pay a change fee.

## Northeast Thanksgiving Day

[Delta's Northeast weather flexibility page](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/weather-alerts/northeast-weather-flexibility) covers travel to, from, or through Baltimore (BWI), Boston (BOS), JFK, LaGuardia (LGA), Newark (EWR), Philadelphia (PHL), Washington Dulles (IAD), Washington Reagan (DCA), and White Plains (HPN) on November 26th only. Tickets must have been issued on or before November 24th. You can change the flight on or before November 28th, and the new departure must also be on or before November 28th, or cancel for an eCredit. Fare differences are waived in the same cabin if that booking class is available. Changes to origin or destination can add a fare difference.

[American's travel alerts page](https://www.aa.com/web/i18n/travel-info/travel-alerts.html) (current as of November 24th) covers American-operated flights in any fare class, including Basic Economy, to, from, or through those same nine airports on November 26th only. Tickets must have been bought by November 23rd. You can travel November 23rd through 29th between the same cities, and stay in the same cabin or pay the difference. Changes must be booked by November 26th.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the airline app or manage booking before you go to the airport.
2. Recheck the [FAA National Airspace System status](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) and the [SFO airport status page](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=SFO&p=1) if you want a ground-stop view that may not show in the app yet.
3. Look for a travel waiver in manage booking before you pay a change fee. If you qualify and your plans are flexible, move while seats remain. American's listed SFO and Miami change deadline is November 25th. The Northeast window's change deadline is November 26th.
4. If the airline canceled your flight, stay on its rebooking path first. Keep receipts for meals or hotels if you get stuck overnight.

Weather delays in the US generally do not trigger a standalone airline cash payment for a simple delay. Under [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds), a cancellation or significant delay is a different conversation if you choose not to travel. If you paid with a card that includes flight-delay insurance, that coverage can still matter for hotel and meal costs after a long enough delay. For a practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Wind is already delaying San Francisco arrivals by more than two hours, and United, American, and Southwest still have fee-free change tools for eligible Wednesday SFO tickets. American's listed SFO and Miami change deadline is November 25th. A coastal storm is also forecast to hit the East Coast on Thanksgiving Day, and Delta and American have a separate November 26th window for nine Northeast airports. Check your flight, and use a waiver in manage booking before you pay change fees.
