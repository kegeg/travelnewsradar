Delta is waiving change fees on eligible tickets to, from, or through six eastern Florida airports on November 19th and 20th after flooding. Eligible tickets can change in the Delta app or My Trips by November 23rd. Thursday evening FAA boards at Miami and Fort Lauderdale showed only light delays.

This is the same check-your-booking habit as [Hurricane Isaias in Florida](/news/pensacola-destin-airports-closed-hurricane-isaias/) and [Northeast storm weekend waivers](/news/northeast-storm-weekend-flight-waivers/).

## What happened

Delta's [Eastern Florida Infrastructure advisory](https://www.delta.com/us/en/advisories/other-alerts/eastern-florida-infrastructure) covers travel to, from, or through Daytona Beach (DAB), Fort Lauderdale (FLL), Jacksonville (JAX), Melbourne, Florida (MLB), Miami (MIA), and West Palm Beach (DJT) on November 19th and 20th. The passenger page cites eastern Florida infrastructure. Delta's [travel-agent bulletin](https://pro.delta.com/content/agency/us/en/news/exception-policy-archive/2026/november-2026/eastern-florida-infrastructure-bulletin-1.html), issued at 10:30 a.m. Eastern on November 19th, says the waiver is for customers who may be affected by flooding and infrastructure.

The [National Weather Service in Miami](https://www.weather.gov/mfl/) still had a Flood Watch through Friday morning, November 20th, for Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. Its Thursday evening forecast discussion said 3 to 5 inches (about 8 to 13 cm) of rain had already fallen along the east coast of South Florida over several days, with pockets of 8 to 15 inches (about 20 to 38 cm). Isolated extra amounts of 3 to 5 inches were still possible. The office expected the widespread flood threat to ease Friday afternoon, with drying this weekend.

The FAA issued a [weather ground stop at Fort Lauderdale](https://www.fly.faa.gov/adv/adv_otherdis?adv_date=11182026&advn=31&facId=FLL&title=ATCSCC+ADVZY+031+FLL%2FZMA+11%2F18%2F2026+CDM+GROUND+STOP&titleDate=11%2F18%2F2026) on Wednesday morning, November 18th, from 8:30 to 9:45 a.m. Eastern. [JetBlue's Fort Lauderdale flooding notice](https://www.jetblue.com/travelalerts), last updated at 10:45 a.m. Eastern Thursday, still warned of delays and road closures around FLL.

As of about 8:24 p.m. Eastern Thursday, the [FAA national status board](https://nasstatus.faa.gov/) was not listing an active ground stop at Miami, Fort Lauderdale, or West Palm Beach. Airport pages at [Miami](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=MIA&p=1), [Fort Lauderdale](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=FLL&p=1), [Jacksonville](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=JAX&p=1), and [West Palm Beach](https://www.fly.faa.gov/fly/flyfaa/flyfaaindex?ARPT=DJT&p=1) showed gate-hold, taxi, and arrival delays of 15 minutes or less. Those pages are not flight-specific.

## What Delta is offering

Open My Trips before you pay a change fee.

| Item | Delta ticket |
| --- | --- |
| Original travel | November 19th and 20th |
| Airports | DAB, FLL, JAX, MLB, MIA, DJT |
| Ticket issued | On or before November 19th |
| Change by | November 23rd |
| New departure | On or before November 23rd |
| Fare difference | Waived in the same cabin if that booking class is available |
| Cancel | eCredit for future travel |

If the original booking class is gone, a fare difference can still apply inside that window. Changes to origin or destination can also add a fare difference. Rebooked travel after November 23rd can cost more.

The bulletin covers Delta and Delta Connection tickets. If you booked through another airline or a package company, go back to that seller.

## What this means for travelers

If you hold a Delta ticket to, from, or through one of those six airports on November 19th or 20th, you can move the trip while that window is still open, as long as the new departure is on or before November 23rd and you stay in the same cabin.

Orlando (MCO) and Tampa (TPA) are not on Delta's city list. Melbourne (MLB) is Melbourne Orlando International in Brevard County, not Orlando International.

If your ticket is on another airline, check that carrier before you pay a change fee. JetBlue has a Fort Lauderdale flooding notice for delays and road access to the airport. American's alerts page did not list a Florida flooding waiver Thursday evening.

Canadian itineraries that connect at Miami or Fort Lauderdale take the same hit as a US domestic ticket.

People already in South Florida should watch flooded roads and extra drive time to the airport, not just the flight status. If you are still booking after this weekend, nothing material should change.

If Delta canceled your flight, stay on its rebooking path first, including a partner flight it assigns.

## What to do

1. Check whether **your flight** is delayed or canceled in the Delta app or My Trips before you leave for the airport.
2. If you qualify and want a different day, change by November 23rd, with the new departure also on or before November 23rd.
3. Recheck road conditions if you are driving to Fort Lauderdale. JetBlue's notice still flagged access delays there Thursday morning.
4. If Delta canceled the flight, take its reaccommodation before you buy a new ticket on another carrier.
5. Keep receipts for meals or hotels if you get stuck overnight.

Weather delays in the US generally do not trigger a standalone airline cash payment for a simple delay. Under [DOT refund rules](https://www.transportation.gov/individuals/aviation-consumer-protection/refunds), a cancellation or significant delay is a different conversation if you choose not to travel. If you paid with a card that includes flight-delay insurance, that coverage can still matter for hotel and meal costs after a long enough delay. For a practical checklist, see [what to do when a flight is canceled or delayed](https://flytrippers.com/6-things-to-do-when-your-flight-is-canceled-or-delayed/).

## Bottom line

Delta posted a fee-free change tool for eligible November 19th and 20th tickets through six eastern Florida airports after flooding. Rebook by November 23rd in the Delta app or My Trips. Thursday evening FAA boards at Miami, Fort Lauderdale, Jacksonville, and West Palm Beach showed only light delays. Check your flight anyway, and use the waiver before you pay a change fee.
